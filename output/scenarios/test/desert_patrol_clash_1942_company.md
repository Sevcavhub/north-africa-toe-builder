# 1. DESERT PATROL CLASH

**Scenario Metadata**
- **Battle Size**: Company
- **Table Size**: 6' × 4'
- **Year**: 1942
- **Location Type**: Open Desert
- **Recommended Nations**: british, german, italian, american

## SITUATION REPORT

Reconnaissance patrols from both sides have unexpectedly encountered each other in the desert. Neither side has had time to prepare positions. The side that can seize the key terrain features and establish control will gain valuable intelligence and tactical advantage.

## TACTICAL SITUATION

This is a fast-moving encounter battle. Scout units are critical for gaining initiative and choosing favorable positions. Rapid deployment and aggressive movement to objectives will determine the victor.

## THE BATTLEFIELD

**Table Size**: 6' × 4'

**Terrain Features** (rolled on North Africa Terrain Table):

1. **Stream/Irrigation Ditch** (Table length, 3" wide)
   - Fordable stream or irrigation canal.
   - *Special Rules*: Difficult terrain for vehicles. May have vegetation along banks (soft cover).
   - *Cover*: None
   - *Movement*: Difficult

2. **Stone Building** (Single building)
   - Small stone structure, typical Arab architecture.
   - *Special Rules*: Infantry gain hard cover. Impassable to vehicles unless destroyed.
   - *Cover*: Hard
   - *Movement*: Impassable

3. **Oasis** (10" × 10" area)
   - Small oasis with palm trees and water. Strategic objective.
   - *Special Rules*: Counts as wood for cover (soft). Usually marks an objective. May include a well.
   - *Cover*: Soft
   - *Movement*: None

**Terrain Notes**: Light terrain typical of desert patrol routes

## WEATHER CONDITIONS

**Condition**: Clear

**Effects**:
- Desert Dust Cloud begins on turn 6
- From turn 6: -1 to observation tests
- From turn 6: Maximum weapon range 40"
- From turn 6: Maximum artillery spotter range 40"

## VICTORY CONDITIONS

**Major Victory**: Control 3+ objectives at game end AND have not reached BR limit.

**Minor Victory**: Control 2+ objectives at game end OR force enemy to BR limit.

**Draw**: Equal objectives controlled or both sides reach BR limit.

## OBJECTIVES

**Number of Objectives**: 4
**First Objective Placement**: Center of table
**Spacing Rules**: All objectives must be 10" from table edges and other objectives
**Placed By**: Defender places first, then alternating

## DEPLOYMENT PROCEDURE

1. Both players count their scout/recce units
2. Player with MOST scouts chooses table edge (tie = roll off)
3. Player with MOST scouts deploys first
4. Both players place scouts first, then remaining units alternately
5. Roll for initiative each turn (scouts give +1 per scout unit)

**Deployment Zones**:
- **Player 1**: South table edge, 12" deep
  - May deploy scouts up to 18" onto table
- **Player 2**: North table edge, 12" deep
  - May deploy scouts up to 18" onto table

## SPECIAL SCENARIO RULES

- **Scout Advantage**: +1 to initiative per scout unit
- **Ambush Fire**: Side with most scouts may have D3 units ambush fire
- **Mobile Warfare**: All vehicles move at full speed on roads/tracks
- **Meeting Engagement**: No prepared positions or fortifications
