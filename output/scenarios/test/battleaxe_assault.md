# 1. HALFAYA PASS ASSAULT
## SITUATION REPORT
**Date**: June 15-17, 1941
**Location**: Halfaya Pass, Libya-Egypt Border

Operation Battleaxe was a British offensive to relieve Tobruk and push back Rommel's forces. The attack on Halfaya Pass was crucial to opening the coastal route. German forces had fortified the pass with 88mm guns and created strong defensive positions.

The British need to break through the Axis defenses at Halfaya Pass to advance along the coast road toward Tobruk. German forces under Generalmajor Bach have created a formidable defensive position with interlocking fields of fire.
## THE BATTLE
The Britishs are launching an assault to capture key objectives. The Germans must hold their positions and repel the attack.

**The Britishs Objective**: Capture the primary objective and break the enemy's will to fight

**The Germans Objective**: Hold the objective and inflict sufficient casualties to stop the assault
## THE BATTLEFIELD
**Table Size**: 6' × 4'

**Terrain**:
- **Objective Building**: Center of table (Hard cover, counts as objective)
- **Woods**: Two woods on flanks, 8" diameter (Soft cover, difficult terrain)
- **Hill**: Defender's table edge, 12" from edge (Elevated position, good fields of fire)

**Special Battlefield Rules**:
- Defender may place up to 3 additional terrain pieces (trenches, obstacles, etc.)
- Buildings provide hard cover

---

## OBJECTIVES
**Victory Type**: mixed

**The Britishs Victory**: Control the primary objective at game end AND have not reached your Battle Rating limit

**The Germans Victory**: Control the primary objective at game end OR force the attacker to reach their Battle Rating limit

**Draw**: Any other result

## DEPLOYMENT
**The Britishs**: Attacker's table edge, up to 12" onto table

**The Germans**: Defender's table edge, up to 18" onto table (prepared positions)

**Turn Order**: Attacker has initiative and goes first

## SPECIAL SCENARIO RULES
- Prepared Positions: Defender's units in cover at start gain +1 to pinning tests
- Assault Doctrine: Attacker's units within 12" of objective gain +1 to assault moves

**Turn Limit**: 8 turns
