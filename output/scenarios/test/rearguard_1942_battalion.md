# 12. REARGUARD ACTION

**Scenario Metadata**
- **Battle Size**: Battalion
- **Table Size**: 6' × 4'
- **Year**: 1942
- **Location Type**: Open Desert
- **Recommended Nations**: british, german, italian, american

## SITUATION REPORT

The main force is withdrawing and your rearguard must buy them time. Enemy forces are pursuing aggressively. Your mission: establish successive delay positions, inflict casualties, slow the pursuit, and extract your forces intact. Every hour of delay is a success.

## TACTICAL SITUATION

This is one of the most difficult missions: fighting while retreating. The rearguard must trade space for time without being decisively engaged. Position, delay, inflict casualties, disengage, fall back, repeat. The pursuer must press hard before the rearguard can organize the next position.

## THE BATTLEFIELD

**Table Size**: 6' × 4'

**Terrain Features** (rolled on North Africa Terrain Table):

1. **Paved Road** (6" wide, table length)
   - Metalled road, often Italian-built.
   - *Special Rules*: Fast movement for vehicles. Key strategic route. Often an objective.
   - *Cover*: None
   - *Movement*: None

2. **Fortified Position** (12" × 12" area)
   - Prepared defensive position with trenches and dugouts.
   - *Special Rules*: Infantry gain hard cover with +1 to cover saves. Difficult terrain for vehicles.
   - *Cover*: Hard
   - *Movement*: Difficult

3. **Escarpment** (Table edge to 12" deep)
   - Steep cliff face or escarpment. Major tactical feature.
   - *Special Rules*: Impassable to vehicles. Infantry can climb (dangerous terrain). Artillery observers gain +1.
   - *Cover*: Hard
   - *Movement*: Impassable

4. **Wadi (Dry)** (20" long, 6" wide)
   - Dry riverbed cutting through desert. Excellent cover and concealment.
   - *Special Rules*: Infantry and vehicles in wadi gain hard cover. Counts as difficult terrain to enter/exit.
   - *Cover*: Hard
   - *Movement*: Difficult

**Terrain Notes**: Mix of terrain providing successive defensive positions

## VICTORY CONDITIONS

**Defender Major Victory**: Exit 75%+ of force off North edge by turn 8.

**Defender Minor Victory**: Exit 50-74% of force off North edge.

**Attacker Victory**: Prevent defender escape (defender exits <50%).

**Draw**: Defender escapes but suffers 50%+ casualties.

## OBJECTIVES

**Number of Objectives**: 3
**First Objective Placement**: Defender's withdrawal route (North edge)
**Spacing Rules**: 3 delay positions across table
**Placed By**: Defender places 3 successive delay positions

## DEPLOYMENT PROCEDURE

1. Defender places 3 successive delay positions (South, Center, North)
2. Defender deploys 1/3 force at each position
3. Attacker deploys at South edge
4. Defender may withdraw units that break contact (move to next position)
5. Game has 8 turn limit - defender wins by surviving
6. Attacker moves first (pursuing)

**Deployment Zones**:
- **Attacker**: South table edge, 12" deep
  - Pursuing retreating enemy
  - Must prevent enemy escape
  - Aggressive advance required
- **Defender (Rearguard)**: Center and North areas (successive positions)
  - Must delay attacker
  - Can withdraw units that break contact
  - Victory = time delay, not holding ground

## SPECIAL SCENARIO RULES

- **Fighting Withdrawal**: Defender may withdraw units to next position if not in contact
- **Successive Positions**: Defender has 3 prepared positions to fall back through
- **Time Limit**: 8 turns - defender wins by exiting units off North edge
- **Exit Points**: Defender scores points for units exited off North edge
- **Pursuit**: Attacker must press hard to prevent organized withdrawal
- **Prepared Positions**: Each defender position starts with defensive prep
