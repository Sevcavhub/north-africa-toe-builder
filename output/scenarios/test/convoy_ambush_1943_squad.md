# 7. SUPPLY CONVOY AMBUSH

**Scenario Metadata**
- **Battle Size**: Squad/Platoon
- **Table Size**: 6' × 4'
- **Year**: 1943
- **Location Type**: Tunisia
- **Recommended Nations**: british, german, italian, american

## SITUATION REPORT

Intelligence reports an enemy supply convoy moving along the desert road. This is an opportunity to strike a critical blow against enemy logistics. Your forces have positioned for an ambush. Destroy or capture the convoy trucks before they escape.

## TACTICAL SITUATION

The ambusher must strike fast and hard before escorts can react. The convoy must keep moving - stopping means destruction. Classic desert warfare: fast raids on vulnerable supply lines.

## THE BATTLEFIELD

**Table Size**: 6' × 4'

**Terrain Features** (rolled on North Africa Terrain Table):

1. **Desert Road/Track** (Runs East-West across table center)
   - Main supply route for the convoy
   - *Special Rules*: Fast movement for vehicles. Convoy must stay on road.
   - *Cover*: None
   - *Movement*: None

2. **Mud-Brick Village** (4-6 buildings, 20" area)
   - Small North African village with adobe houses.
   - *Special Rules*: Buildings provide hard cover. Narrow streets are difficult terrain for vehicles.
   - *Cover*: Hard
   - *Movement*: Difficult

3. **Salt Flat (Sabkha)** (15" × 15" area)
   - Dried salt lake bed. Treacherous for vehicles.
   - *Special Rules*: Looks flat but is dangerous terrain for vehicles (risk of breakthrough). Infantry move normally.
   - *Cover*: None
   - *Movement*: Dangerous

4. **Rocky Desert** (20" × 20" area)
   - Desert floor scattered with rocks and stones.
   - *Special Rules*: Vehicles treat as difficult terrain. Infantry move normally.
   - *Cover*: None
   - *Movement*: Difficult

5. **Fortified Position** (12" × 12" area)
   - Prepared defensive position with trenches and dugouts.
   - *Special Rules*: Infantry gain hard cover with +1 to cover saves. Difficult terrain for vehicles.
   - *Cover*: Hard
   - *Movement*: Difficult

**Terrain Notes**: Open terrain with limited cover near the road

## WEATHER CONDITIONS

**Condition**: Rain

**Effects**:
- All aircraft grounded (Air Attack counters automatically fail)
- All off-road terrain counts as difficult
- Tracks become muddy (difficult terrain)
- Visibility normal (rain, not dust)

## VICTORY CONDITIONS

**Attacker Victory**: Destroy or capture 2+ convoy trucks before they escape.

**Defender Victory**: Get 2+ trucks off East edge OR destroy ambush force.

**Draw**: Equal trucks destroyed/escaped, or convoy halted.

## OBJECTIVES

**Number of Objectives**: 3
**First Objective Placement**: Convoy trucks (defender controlled)
**Spacing Rules**: Trucks start on road in defender's deployment zone
**Placed By**: Defender places convoy trucks on road

## DEPLOYMENT PROCEDURE

1. Place road running E-W across table
2. Defender places 3-4 convoy trucks on road at West edge
3. Defender deploys escort forces within 6" of convoy
4. Attacker places hidden deployment markers (reveal when acting)
5. Convoy begins moving East on turn 1
6. Ambush is sprung when attacker reveals or defender spots them

**Deployment Zones**:
- **Attacker (Ambush Force)**: Hidden deployment within 18" of road (excluding defender zone)
  - Deploy hidden (reveal when moving or firing)
  - May deploy after seeing convoy positions
  - First turn surprise (+1 to hit)
- **Defender (Convoy)**: West table edge moving East along road
  - Convoy trucks must be on road
  - Escorts deploy within 6" of trucks
  - Convoy moves first turn toward East edge

## SPECIAL SCENARIO RULES

- **Convoy Movement**: Trucks move 12" per turn along road toward East edge
- **Hidden Deployment**: Attacker deploys hidden, reveals when moving/firing
- **Surprise**: First turn of ambush, attacker gets +1 to hit
- **Truck Vulnerability**: Convoy trucks have Armor 0, destroyed on any penetrating hit
- **Escape**: Trucks that exit East edge are saved (count as objectives held)
- **Escort**: Defender must keep escorts within 12" of convoy or trucks stop
