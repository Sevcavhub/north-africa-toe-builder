# 8. AIRFIELD ASSAULT

**Scenario Metadata**
- **Battle Size**: Company
- **Table Size**: 6' × 4'
- **Year**: 1943
- **Location Type**: Tunisia
- **Recommended Nations**: british, german, italian, american

## SITUATION REPORT

An enemy airfield lies lightly defended. A bold raid could destroy aircraft on the ground and cripple enemy air operations. Your forces must strike fast, destroy as much as possible, and withdraw before enemy reinforcements arrive from the main base.

## TACTICAL SITUATION

This is a raid, not an occupation. Speed is essential. Destroy high-value targets (aircraft, fuel) quickly and get out. Don't get bogged down or you'll be trapped when reinforcements arrive.

## THE BATTLEFIELD

**Table Size**: 6' × 4'

**Terrain Features** (rolled on North Africa Terrain Table):

1. **Desert Airstrip** (Center of table, 30" × 20" area)
   - Cleared runway with fuel dumps and aircraft dispersals
   - *Special Rules*: Open ground. Aircraft and fuel dumps are objectives/targets.
   - *Cover*: None
   - *Movement*: None

2. **Hangar/Control Tower** (North side of runway)
   - Small buildings for airfield operations
   - *Special Rules*: Hard cover. May be fortified by defender.
   - *Cover*: Hard
   - *Movement*: Impassable

3. **Fuel Dump** (East side of runway)
   - Stacked fuel drums (target)
   - *Special Rules*: Soft cover. Explodes if destroyed (pinning within 10").
   - *Cover*: Soft
   - *Movement*: None

4. **Aircraft Dispersals** (Scatter 3-4 around runway)
   - Parked aircraft (targets)
   - *Special Rules*: Destroying aircraft is attacker objective.
   - *Cover*: None
   - *Movement*: None

**Terrain Notes**: Open airfield with parked aircraft and fuel as primary targets

## WEATHER CONDITIONS

**Condition**: Clear

**Effects**:
- Clear weather. No special effects.

## VICTORY CONDITIONS

**Attacker Major Victory**: Destroy 3+ aircraft AND fuel dump AND withdraw by turn 6.

**Attacker Minor Victory**: Destroy 2+ aircraft or fuel dump AND withdraw.

**Defender Victory**: Prevent destruction of aircraft OR trap attacker (don't let them withdraw).

**Draw**: Heavy damage but both sides depleted.

## OBJECTIVES

**Number of Objectives**: 5
**First Objective Placement**: Destroy/capture airfield facilities
**Spacing Rules**: 3-4 aircraft + fuel dump + control tower = 5 objectives
**Placed By**: Scenario-defined (airfield facilities)

## DEPLOYMENT PROCEDURE

1. Place airfield runway in table center
2. Place 3-4 aircraft at dispersals around runway
3. Place fuel dump, hangar, control tower
4. Defender deploys light garrison (50% points) within 12" of runway
5. Attacker deploys entire force in deployment zone
6. Attacker gets first turn (surprise raid)

**Deployment Zones**:
- **Attacker (Raiding Force)**: South table edge, 12" deep
  - Fast moving raid force
  - Must destroy aircraft and facilities
  - May need to withdraw before reinforcements arrive
- **Defender (Airfield Garrison)**: Within 12" of runway/buildings
  - Light garrison forces (50% of attacker points)
  - Reinforcements arrive turn 3+ (main base nearby)
  - May fortify control tower

## SPECIAL SCENARIO RULES

- **Aircraft Targets**: Each aircraft destroyed = 1 objective. Armor 0, any hit destroys.
- **Fuel Dump**: Explodes on destruction (all units within 10" must pass pinning test)
- **Control Tower**: Capturing control tower = 1 objective (must have unit in base)
- **Time Limit**: Attacker must withdraw by turn 6 or face overwhelming reinforcements
- **Surprise Raid**: Attacker gets +1 to hit on turn 1 only
- **Light Garrison**: Defender starts with 50% points, reinforcements 50% arrive turn 3+

## REINFORCEMENTS

**Defender**:
- Starting Turn: 3
- Dice per Turn: 2D6
- Special Rules:
  - Roll 2D6 at start of turn 3+
  - On a 4+, reinforcements arrive from reserve edge
  - Maximum 1/3 of force may arrive per turn
  - Reinforcements are base reaction force arriving from North edge
