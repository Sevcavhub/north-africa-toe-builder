Maintained as mobile infantry force under British Eighth Army command, positioned in rear areas for reorganization.

- Battle-hardened veterans of Bir Hakeim siege (May 26-June 11, 1942)

- National WWII Museum - 'Forgotten Fights: The Free French at Bir Hacheim, May 1942' (https://www.nationalww2museum.org/war/articles/free-french-bir-hacheim-1942) - Brigade strength (~4,000 personnel under Brigadier General Koenig), composition (French Marines, Foreign Legionnaires, soldiers from Senegal, Madagascar, Central Africa), combat effectiveness (32 enemy tanks knocked out), breakout success (majority of garrison escaped June 10-11)
- French Foreign Legion Information - 'History of the 13th Foreign Legion Demi-Brigade' (http://foreignlegion.info/history/13dble/) - 13e DBLE reorganization after Bir Hakeim: two battalions (2nd and 3rd) assigned to Koenig's brigade December 1941; suffered huge losses at Bir Hakeim requiring merger into single battalion (2nd Battalion); later rejoined by reconstituted 1st Battalion; participated in Second Battle of El Alamein under Lt. Col. Amilakvari
- Ordre de la Libération (French state institution) - 'Dimitri AMILAKVARI' (https://www.ordredelaliberation.fr/es/node/1263) - 13e DBLE command structure (Amilakvari promoted to Lt. Colonel September 1941, commanded three-battalion demi-brigade reinforced by ~1,000 légionnaires from 6e REI); Bir Hakeim casualties (141 dead, 229 wounded, 814 prisoners, 53 guns and 50 vehicles lost); El Alamein October 1942 force structure (13e DBLE reduced to two battalions, Amilakvari killed in action October 24, 1942)
- France24 - 'La bataille de Bir Hakeim a accru la confiance des Alliés dans la France libre' (https://www.france24.com/fr/france/20220527-la-bataille-de-bir-hakeim-a-accru-la-confiance-des-alliés-dans-la-france-libre) - Artillery losses documented: 1er RAFFL lost 64 men including 7 officers; of 24 guns, only 8 remained (16 guns lost); regiment was sole artillery at Bir Hakeim with four batteries of six 75mm guns; post-battle reorganization with English equipment (four batteries with four 88mm guns and one 140mm battery)
