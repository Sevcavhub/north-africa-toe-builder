## 11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

### Doctrinal Role

Defensive static infantry division assigned to hold critical Mareth Line positions. Despite 'Armoured' designation, functioned as standard infantry division with minimal mobile capability. Primary mission: Hold defensive line against British Eighth Army advance from Libya.

### Special Capabilities

- Politically motivated troops (Fascist youth volunteers) with high ideological commitment
- Bersaglieri component provided some mobile infantry capability
- Portee artillery (truck-mounted guns) allowed rapid repositioning within defensive zone

### Combined Arms Integration

**[MANUAL: Add section about infantry-tank-artillery cooperation, air support, engineer support]**

### Known Issues and Limitations

- Never received promised tank battalions - misnomer designation
- Insufficient training - many volunteers were university students with minimal military experience
- Chronic equipment shortages throughout existence
- Low combat effectiveness compared to regular army divisions
- Poor coordination between regular army and MVSN components
- Inadequate anti-tank capability for 1943 battlefield
- Fuel shortages severely limited mobility of motorized elements

### Desert Adaptations

Division formed too late to develop extensive desert warfare experience. Tunisia campaign (Jan-May 1943) fought in Mediterranean coastal terrain, not pure desert. Limited water transport capacity. Minimal experience with sandstorm operations.
