## 1. Overview

The 63ª Divisione di Fanteria "Cirene" stood as one of Italy's pre-war metropolitan garrison divisions defending Eastern Cyrenaica when Italy entered World War II on June 10, 1940. With 12,169 soldiers permanently stationed in Libya, Cirene Division manned defensive positions along the Egyptian border. The division exemplified Italy's binary infantry division structure implemented March 1940—reducing regiments from three to two.

Unlike autotrasportabile divisions (Pavia, Brescia), Cirene remained a **foot-mobile division** with only 145 trucks, dependent on animal transport. The division's two infantry regiments (157° and 158° Reggimento "LIGURIA") plus attached Legione CC.NN. "Cirene" (1,305 militia) represented binary organization with political commissar complications.
