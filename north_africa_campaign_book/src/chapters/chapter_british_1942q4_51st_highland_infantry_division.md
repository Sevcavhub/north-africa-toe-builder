## 11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

### Doctrinal Role

Assault infantry division specialized in breakthrough operations

### Special Capabilities

- Night assault operations
- Mine clearance and breaching operations
- Infantry-tank cooperation (with attached armoured brigades)
- Highland fighting spirit and esprit de corps
- Aggressive assault tactics

### Tactical Innovations

- Night assault with compass bearings (El Alamein 'Crumbling' phase)
- Coordinated infantry-engineer-tank teams for minefield breaching
- Use of bagpipes and Highland regimental traditions to boost morale
- Insistence on Scottish identity and regimental pride ('Tartan Tam' leadership)

### Combined Arms Integration

**[MANUAL: Add section about infantry-tank-artillery cooperation, air support, engineer support]**

### Known Issues and Limitations

- First battle for reformed division - green troops with limited combat experience
- Sustained ~2,000 casualties at El Alamein (11% of strength)
- Infantry division lacking mobility compared to armoured formations
- Dependence on attached armoured brigades for mobile operations

### Desert Adaptations

Sun compasses, water discipline, dust protection, desert camouflage. Division arrived Egypt August 1942, had only 2 months desert acclimatization before El Alamein.
