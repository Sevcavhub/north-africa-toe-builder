## 11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

### Doctrinal Role

Armored exploitation and breakthrough force. Heavy armored division optimized for sustained mobile operations with emphasis on tank firepower and shock action.

### Special Capabilities

- Three-battalion armored regiments provide greater striking power than light armored divisions
- Combat Command structure allows flexible combined arms task organization
- Self-propelled artillery maintains pace with armored advance
- Mechanized infantry provides immediate infantry support without dismounting

### Tactical Innovations

- Combat Command system pioneered flexible task force organization
- Integration of self-propelled artillery with armored maneuver elements
- Reconnaissance battalion provides deep reconnaissance capability

### Combined Arms Integration

**[MANUAL: Add section about infantry-tank-artillery cooperation, air support, engineer support]**

### Known Issues and Limitations

- Division too large and unwieldy for effective control (would be reorganized 1943)
- Tank distribution across two regiments dispersed firepower
- M3 Lee/Grant tanks inferior to German Panzer IV in Tunisia
- Inexperienced crews and commanders in first combat operations
- Supply and maintenance units struggled with North African conditions
- Dispersal of Combat Commands by higher headquarters reduced division effectiveness

### Desert Adaptations

Division still adapting to North African conditions in December 1942. Issues with sand filters, water supply, navigation, and maintenance in austere environment. Medjez el Bab disaster (10-11 December) highlighted vulnerability to mud and weather in rainy season.
