Italy's premier armored division, designed for mobile warfare and breakthrough operations

- Motorized infantry for rapid deployment
- Motorcycle battalion for reconnaissance and exploitation
- First Italian division equipped with M13/40 medium tanks
- Combined arms coordination between tanks, infantry, and artillery

### Tactical Innovations

- Integration of Bersaglieri motorized infantry with armored units
- Mixed tank battalions with different vehicle types for varied roles

### Known Issues and Limitations

- L3/35 tankettes obsolete by 1940 standards, poorly armed and armored
- M13/40 tanks still in limited production, only one battalion fully equipped
- Most tank battalions transferred separately to Libya, division not at full strength
- Limited radio equipment (only 3 radios per 37-tank battalion)
- Artillery lacks modern fire control systems
- Trucks and vehicles not fully desert-adapted in Q4 1940

### Desert Adaptations

Division still in Italy in Q4 1940, preparing for Libya deployment. Desert modifications not yet applied. Full division would arrive Libya January 1941.
