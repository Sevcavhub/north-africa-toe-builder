## 8. Supply and Logistics

### Supply Status

Reserve corps positioned at Tobruk/Sofafi. Better supply situation than forward corps at Sidi Barrani due to proximity to supply base. Fuel reserves adequate for defensive operations (12-15 days), ammunition stocks good (18-20 days), water supply from coastal wells adequate. However, XXI Corps held in reserve during Italian invasion of Egypt (Sept 1940) and not committed to Sidi Barrani fortified camps. When Operation Compass launched (9 Dec 1940), XXI Corps divisions were better positioned but still suffered from overall 10th Army command chaos and logistics collapse.

## 11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

### Doctrinal Role

Reserve corps for 10th Army. Positioned at Tobruk/Sofafi to support forward divisions at Sidi Barrani. NOT committed to isolated fortified camps that were destroyed in Operation Compass opening.

### Special Capabilities

- Reserve status - better supplied than forward corps
- Positioned at Tobruk port - direct access to sea supply route
- Mixed metropolitan (Cirene) and Blackshirt militia divisions

### Known Issues and Limitations

- Blackshirt militia divisions generally lower quality than metropolitan divisions
- Not committed to action during Italian invasion of Egypt (Sept 1940)
- Held in reserve during disastrous Sidi Barrani deployment (Sept-Dec 1940)
- Command chaos when Operation Compass launched - corps unable to effectively support forward divisions
- Insufficient motor transport for rapid redeployment

### Desert Adaptations
