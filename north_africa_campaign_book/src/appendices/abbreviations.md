# Abbreviations

## Nations and Forces

- **DAK** - Deutsches Afrikakorps (German Africa Corps)
- **WDF** - Western Desert Force (British)
- **2nd AIF** - 2nd Australian Imperial Force
- **NZEF** - New Zealand Expeditionary Force
- **FFL** - Forces Françaises Libres (Free French Forces)

## British/Commonwealth

- **RASC** - Royal Army Service Corps
- **RAMC** - Royal Army Medical Corps
- **RIASC** - Royal Indian Army Service Corps
- **RAC** - Royal Armoured Corps

## German

- **OKH** - Oberkommando des Heeres (Army High Command)
- **OKW** - Oberkommando der Wehrmacht (Armed Forces High Command)
- **Pz** - Panzer (tank)
- **Kfz** - Kraftfahrzeug (motor vehicle)
- **SdKfz** - Sonderkraftfahrzeug (special purpose vehicle)

## Italian

- **CC.NN.** - Camicie Nere (Blackshirts militia)
- **MVSN** - Milizia Volontaria per la Sicurezza Nazionale

## Equipment

- **2-pdr** - 2-pounder (40mm AT gun)
- **25-pdr** - 25-pounder (87.6mm field gun)
- **88mm** - 88mm Flak/AT gun
- **PaK** - Panzerabwehrkanone (AT gun)
- **FlaK** - Fliegerabwehrkanone (AA gun)

## Logistics

- **POL** - Petrol, Oil, Lubricants
- **MT** - Motor Transport
- **Tn** - Ton
- **Cwt** - Hundredweight (112 pounds)
