# Glossary

## Military Terms

**TO&E** - Table of Organization and Equipment. Standard organizational structure and equipment allocation for military units.

**Echelon** - Level of military organization (division, brigade, battalion, etc.)

**Establishment** - Authorized strength and equipment of a unit

**Order of Battle (OOB)** - Complete listing of military units and their organization

**Operational Radius** - Maximum distance a unit can operate from its supply base

## Unit Designations

**Division** - Major tactical formation (10,000-20,000 personnel)

**Brigade** - Tactical formation of 3-5 battalions (3,000-5,000 personnel)

**Regiment** - Battalion-level or brigade-level formation depending on nation

**Battalion** - Basic tactical unit (500-1,000 personnel)

**Company** - Subunit of battalion (100-200 personnel)

## Equipment Categories

**AFV** - Armoured Fighting Vehicle (tanks, armored cars)

**AT** - Anti-Tank (guns, rifles designed to destroy armor)

**AA** - Anti-Aircraft (guns designed to engage aircraft)

**SPG** - Self-Propelled Gun (artillery on tracked/wheeled chassis)

**HMG** - Heavy Machine Gun (sustained fire support weapon)

**LMG** - Light Machine Gun (squad automatic weapon)
