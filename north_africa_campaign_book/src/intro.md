# North Africa Campaign 1940-1943

## Complete Order of Battle Database

This comprehensive reference work documents the complete order of battle for all major formations that fought in the North African theater during World War II, from Italy's entry into the war (June 1940) through the final Allied victory in Tunisia (May 1943).

### Coverage

- **117 unique units** across all nations
- **689 unit-quarter chapters** with detailed TO&E data
- **23 quarterly air summaries** (1941-1943)
- **All major battles**: Operation Compass, Operation Battleaxe, Operation Crusader, Gazala, El Alamein, Operation Torch, Tunisia Campaign

### Nations Covered

- **British/Commonwealth**: 154 unit-quarters (India, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, Poland, Greece)
- **Italian**: 147 unit-quarters
- **German**: 59 unit-quarters (Deutsches Afrikakorps, Panzerarmee Afrika)
- **American**: 23 unit-quarters (Operation Torch through Tunisia)
- **French**: 19 unit-quarters (Free French Forces)

### Data Quality

- **Tier 1/2 sources only**: Official histories, war diaries, archival documents
- **Zero Wikipedia references**: All data from primary/secondary military sources
- **Confidence ratings**: 75-95% across all units
- **Schema v3.0.0**: Includes supply/logistics and environmental data

### Organization

This book is organized into three main sections:

1. **Quarter Overviews** (13 quarters): Strategic situation, major operations, weather, supply status
2. **Ground Forces** (689 chapters): Division/brigade/corps-level units with complete TO&E
3. **Air Forces** (23 summaries): Theater-wide air strength by nation and quarter

Each unit chapter includes:
- Command structure and personnel
- Complete equipment inventories
- Supply and logistics status
- Environmental conditions
- Tactical doctrine and capabilities
- Historical context and significance
- Data validation and sources

### Usage

This reference is designed for:
- Wargame scenario designers
- Military historians
- Researchers studying WWII logistics and organization
- Modders creating historical simulations
