## Supply & Logistics

**Supply Status**: CATASTROPHIC throughout Q4 1942. Mediterranean supply crisis reached peak during El Alamein period. British interdiction (Royal Navy, RAF Malta-based bombers, ULTRA intelligence) sank majority of supply convoys. Critical tankers Tripolino and Ostia sunk 1 November, strangling fuel supply. Supply line 1,400km from Tripoli to El Alamein front exposed to constant air attack. Rommel's entire Panzerarmee estimated only 3-4 days fuel at start of El Alamein battle (23 Oct). Ammunition stocks critically low: artillery rationed 10-15 rounds/gun/day (vs. British unlimited fire). Water supply barely adequate at 4.5L/man/day (medical requirement 10-15L in desert heat). Food situation slightly better due to captured British supplies but convoy losses reduced reserves. Supply crisis primary factor in Axis defeat at El Alamein and prevented effective defense during retreat. By December, supply base shifted to Tunis but situation remained critical.
