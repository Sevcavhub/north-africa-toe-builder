## Supply & Logistics

**Supply Status**: Critical shortage by Q4 1942. XXI Corps positioned in northern El Alamein sector with catastrophically inadequate supplies. Axis supply crisis from 1800km line from Tripoli meant fuel, ammunition, food, water all critically short. Trento Division on exposed Miteirya Ridge had fuel for 2 days, ammunition for 3.5 days by October. Bologna Division marched 400 miles from Gazala to El Alamein (July) lacking vehicles, arrived exhausted with minimal supplies. By 25 October, Trento lost most artillery to British bombardment, Bologna retreating on foot through desert. Supply failure was primary cause of corps destruction.
