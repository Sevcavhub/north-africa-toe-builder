## Supply & Logistics

**Supply Status**: CHRONICALLY INADEQUATE - Italian logistics struggled throughout North Africa campaign. British Mediterranean interdiction (Malta-based RAF/RN) sank 15-22% of Axis convoys Q1 1942. Even supplies reaching Tripoli faced 1,400 km journey to Gazala line (or 800 km from Benghazi). Italian trucks consumed 30-40% of fuel cargo making delivery runs. German units received priority allocation - DAK operated at 85-95% supply while Ariete at 60-70%. Fuel reserves typically 4-6 days (versus 7-10 days for German units). Ammunition rationed - Italian artillery fired fraction of British volumes. Spare parts catastrophically short, especially tank transmissions and engines. Tank operational readiness only 60-65% (vs 80-85% for German panzer divisions). Captured British supplies at Benghazi (29 Jan 1942) provided temporary relief but convoy losses resumed.
