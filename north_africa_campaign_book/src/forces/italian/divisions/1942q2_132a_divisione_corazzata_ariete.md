## Supply & Logistics

**Supply Status**: STRAINED BUT IMPROVING - Static defensive period (April-May 1942) allowed supply build-up for planned Gazala offensive. British Mediterranean interdiction remained severe - convoy losses averaged 18-25% Q2 1942. Supply line Tripoli-Benghazi-Gazala line stretched 1,200-1,400 km. Italian trucks still consumed 30-40% of fuel cargo making delivery runs. German units continued receiving priority - DAK at 90% supply vs Ariete at 70-75%. Pre-offensive stockpiling (May 1942) raised fuel reserves to 7-8 days, ammunition to 9-10 days (vs 5/7 in Q1). Spare parts situation slightly improved with focused workshop efforts during static period. Tank operational readiness improved to 75-80% (vs 65% Q1). Capture of Tobruk (21 June 1942) yielded massive British supply dumps: 2,000 tons food, 1,400 tons fuel, 5,000 tons ammunition - temporarily eased crisis. However, renewed mobile operations June 1942 consumption rates quickly depleted stocks. By end Q2 (30 June 1942) advancing toward Egypt, division again operating on logistical knife-edge.
