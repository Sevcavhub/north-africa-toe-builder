## Supply & Logistics

**Supply Status**: Critical. Division severely strained after Gazala offensive (May-June 1942) and First El Alamein defensive battles (July 1942). Supply lines stretched 550km from Benghazi, 250km from Tobruk (both under British air interdiction). Mediterranean convoy losses from Malta-based aircraft and submarines devastating. Fuel critically short, limiting tactical movement despite full motorization. Ammunition expenditure in defensive artillery duels exceeds resupply rate. Water supply critical in summer heat (42°C peaks). Italian supply priority below German units.
