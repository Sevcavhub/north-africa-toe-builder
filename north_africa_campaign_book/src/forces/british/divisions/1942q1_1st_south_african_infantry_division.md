## Supply & Logistics

**Supply Status**: Improved from Q4 1941. Division established in defensive positions along northern sector of Gazala Line with static supply depots. Primary supply route from Tobruk (35km) and Benghazi (250km). Water supply adequate from coastal wells and desalination. Division was 5,570 men under-strength as of 8 January 1942 (17,617 vs authorized 23,187). Supply situation stable during defensive preparations but constrained by limited port capacity at Tobruk and long haul from Egypt.
