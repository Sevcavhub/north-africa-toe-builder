# 1941-Q2: Operation Battleaxe (April-June 1941)

## Strategic Situation

Deutsches Afrikakorps (Rommel) arrived March 1941, recaptured Cyrenaica, besieged Tobruk. British launched Operation Battleaxe (June 15-17) to relieve Tobruk using newly-arrived tank reinforcements (Operation Tiger convoy: 238 tanks).

## Major Operations

### June 15: Day 1 - Halfaya Pass/Fort Capuzzo
- British 7th Armoured Brigade attacks Halfaya Pass
- **German defense**: 88mm Flak guns in anti-tank role devastate Matilda IIs
- Fort Capuzzo captured by 4th Indian Division
- British lose 50+ tanks to 88mm guns (first encounter)

### June 16: Day 2 - Armor Battle at Halfaya
- 15. Panzer-Division counterattacks
- British 7th Armoured Division vs. German armor
- Panzer III 50mm gun effective vs. British Cruisers
- British tank losses mount: 91 tanks lost

### June 17: Day 3 - British Withdrawal
- Rommel envelopment threat forces British retreat
- **Results**: Operation failed, Tobruk siege continues
- British losses: 91 tanks, 969 casualties
- German losses: 12 tanks, 678 casualties

## Forces Engaged

**British Commonwealth**:
- 7th Armoured Division (200+ tanks)
- 4th Indian Division (infantry)
- 4th Armoured Brigade
- 7th Armoured Brigade (Matilda II heavy tanks)

**German/Italian**:
- Deutsches Afrikakorps: 15. Panzer-Division, 5. leichte Division
- 88mm Flak guns (anti-tank role)
- Italian Ariete Division, Trento Division

## Tactical Lessons

**German 88mm Flak**: Proved devastating in anti-tank role
- 88mm penetrates Matilda II 78mm armor at 2,000m
- British 2-pounder (40mm) ineffective vs. Panzer III front (50mm)
- German combined arms: 88mm overwatch, Panzer III mobile

**British Weaknesses**:
- Inadequate anti-tank guns (2-pdr ineffective vs. Panzer III)
- Poor tank-infantry coordination
- Dispersed attacks (not concentrated)

## Historical Significance

Operation Battleaxe demonstrated German tactical superiority despite British tank numerical advantage. 88mm Flak in AT role became legendary. British would not attempt Tobruk relief until Operation Crusader (November 1941).

---
*Rommel's defensive victory ensured 6-month Tobruk siege continuation*
