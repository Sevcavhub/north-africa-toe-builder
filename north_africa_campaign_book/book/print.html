North Africa Campaign 1940-1943: Complete Order of Battle

North Africa Campaign 1940-1943

Complete Order of Battle Database

This comprehensive reference work documents the complete order of battle for all major formations that fought in the North African theater during World War II, from Italy's entry into the war (June 1940) through the final Allied victory in Tunisia (May 1943).

Coverage

  • 117 unique units across all nations
  • 689 unit-quarter chapters with detailed TO&E data
  • 23 quarterly air summaries (1941-1943)
  • All major battles: Operation Compass, Operation Battleaxe, Operation Crusader, Gazala, El Alamein, Operation Torch, Tunisia Campaign

Nations Covered

  • British/Commonwealth: 154 unit-quarters (India, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, Poland, Greece)
  • Italian: 147 unit-quarters
  • German: 59 unit-quarters (Deutsches Afrikakorps, Panzerarmee Afrika)
  • American: 23 unit-quarters (Operation Torch through Tunisia)
  • French: 19 unit-quarters (Free French Forces)

Data Quality

  • Tier 1/2 sources only: Official histories, war diaries, archival documents
  • Zero Wikipedia references: All data from primary/secondary military sources
  • Confidence ratings: 75-95% across all units
  • Schema v3.0.0: Includes supply/logistics and environmental data

Organization

This book is organized into three main sections:

  1. Quarter Overviews (13 quarters): Strategic situation, major operations, weather, supply status
  2. Ground Forces (689 chapters): Division/brigade/corps-level units with complete TO&E
  3. Air Forces (23 summaries): Theater-wide air strength by nation and quarter

Each unit chapter includes:

  • Command structure and personnel
  • Complete equipment inventories
  • Supply and logistics status
  • Environmental conditions
  • Tactical doctrine and capabilities
  • Historical context and significance
  • Data validation and sources

Usage

This reference is designed for:

  • Wargame scenario designers
  • Military historians
  • Researchers studying WWII logistics and organization
  • Modders creating historical simulations

Methodology

Data Collection

All data in this reference work was extracted from Tier 1 and Tier 2 primary sources:

Tier 1 Sources

  • Official military histories (British, German, Italian, American official series)
  • War diaries and unit records (National Archives, Bundesarchiv)
  • Contemporary military manuals and technical documentation
  • Memoirs by senior commanders with archival verification

Tier 2 Sources

  • Peer-reviewed academic journals
  • Museum archives and collections
  • Cross-referenced secondary sources with primary documentation

Excluded Sources

  • Wikipedia and general encyclopedias
  • Unverified web content
  • Sources without primary documentation

Data Quality Standards

Each unit chapter includes:

  • Confidence rating: 75-95% based on source quality
  • Source citations: Complete bibliography with archive references
  • Known gaps: Documented limitations and missing data
  • Cross-references: Multiple independent source verification

Schema Compliance

All data follows unified TO&E schema v3.0.0:

  • Personnel (officers, NCOs, enlisted)
  • Equipment (vehicles, artillery, aircraft)
  • Supply and logistics (fuel, ammunition, water, operational radius)
  • Environmental conditions (weather, terrain, seasonal impacts)

Validation Process

  1. Extract data from primary sources
  2. Cross-reference with independent sources
  3. Validate against known historical outcomes
  4. Calculate derived values from standard establishments
  5. Document gaps and uncertainties

All data validated to 75%+ confidence using Tier 1/2 sources only

Glossary

Military Terms

TO&E - Table of Organization and Equipment. Standard organizational structure and equipment allocation for military units.

Echelon - Level of military organization (division, brigade, battalion, etc.)

Establishment - Authorized strength and equipment of a unit

Order of Battle (OOB) - Complete listing of military units and their organization

Operational Radius - Maximum distance a unit can operate from its supply base

Unit Designations

Division - Major tactical formation (10,000-20,000 personnel)

Brigade - Tactical formation of 3-5 battalions (3,000-5,000 personnel)

Regiment - Battalion-level or brigade-level formation depending on nation

Battalion - Basic tactical unit (500-1,000 personnel)

Company - Subunit of battalion (100-200 personnel)

Equipment Categories

AFV - Armoured Fighting Vehicle (tanks, armored cars)

AT - Anti-Tank (guns, rifles designed to destroy armor)

AA - Anti-Aircraft (guns designed to engage aircraft)

SPG - Self-Propelled Gun (artillery on tracked/wheeled chassis)

HMG - Heavy Machine Gun (sustained fire support weapon)

LMG - Light Machine Gun (squad automatic weapon)

See individual chapters for nation-specific terminology
