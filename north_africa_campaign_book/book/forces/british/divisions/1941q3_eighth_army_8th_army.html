Eighth Army (8th Army) (1941Q3)

Personnel

Subordinate Units

XIII Corps

  • Commander: Lieutenant-General Alfred Reade Godwin-Austen
  • Strength: 45,000 personnel
  • Tank Strength: 130 tanks
  • Composition: Infantry-heavy corps. Previously Western Desert Force (redesignated 1 September 1941). Experienced desert fighters. Assigned to southern flank for Operation Crusader.

XXX Corps

  • Commander: Lieutenant-General Vyvyan Vavasour Pope (killed 5 October 1941 Q4)
  • Strength: 36,650 personnel
  • Tank Strength: 172 tanks
  • Composition: Armoured strike corps. Formed August 1941. 7th Armoured Division rebuilt after Battleaxe with new Crusader tanks. Assigned to main armoured thrust for Operation Crusader. Commander Pope killed in air crash 5 October (Q4), succeeded by Lt-Gen Willoughby Norrie.

Tobruk Garrison (attached)

  • Commander: Major-General Leslie Morshead (9th Australian Division)
  • Strength: 25,000 personnel
  • Composition: Under siege since April 1941. Held by 9th Australian Division (replaced late Q3/early Q4 by 70th Division and Polish brigade). Not operationally under Eighth Army but coordination essential for Operation Crusader relief operation.

Army Headquarters

  • Commander: Lieutenant-General Sir Alan Cunningham
  • Strength: 1,215 personnel
  • Composition: Newly-formed army headquarters. Main HQ Cairo with Advanced HQ near Sidi Barrani. BGS Brigadier John Harding key staff officer for Operation Crusader planning.
