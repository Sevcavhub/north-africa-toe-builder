Supply & Logistics

Supply Status: Well-supplied during Operation Compass preparation (October-November 1940). Division had priority for fuel/ammunition as assault formation. Water supply critical - dedicated water truck convoys. Captured Italian rations supplemented British stocks after Nibeiwa/Tummar. Division withdrawal on 11 December relieved supply strain but demonstrated logistical constraints on sustained desert operations.

  • Operational Radius: 150 miles from railhead or main supply dumps with organic RASC transport. Mersa Matruh railhead to Nibeiwa assembly areas: ~80 miles. RASC truck columns ran continuous shuttles during Operation Compass.
