Subordinate Units

4th Indian Infantry Division

  • Commander: Major-General Francis I.S. Tuker
  • Strength: 15,200 personnel
  • Composition: Mountain warfare specialists. Broke through Djebel Fatnassa at Wadi Akarit (6 April) using silent night attack. Two-brigade strength (5th and 7th Brigades - 11th Brigade not yet reformed). Transferred to First Army sector 30 April, participated in final Tunisia offensive (Operation Strike, 6-13 May). 1st Royal Sussex (7th Brigade) accepted surrender of General von Arnim (12 May).

50th (Northumbrian) Infantry Division

  • Commander: Major-General John Nichols
  • Strength: 17,600 personnel
  • Composition: Led Wadi Akarit assault in central sector (69th Brigade). Attacked between 4th Indian Division and Djebel Roumana (5-6 April). Light casualties compared to Mareth assault (March). Withdrawn from line 19 April, moved to Alexandria 24 April for rest and refit after 6 months continuous combat (November 1942-April 1943). Prepared for Sicily invasion (Operation Husky, July 1943).

51st (Highland) Infantry Division

  • Commander: Major-General Douglas Neil Wimberley
  • Strength: 17,850 personnel
  • Composition: Elite Highland division. Assaulted Djebel Roumana feature at Wadi Akarit (152nd and 154th Brigades, 5-6 April). Heavy casualties from German 15th Panzer Division counter-attacks but held positions. Described as 'fiercest fighting to date' for division. After Tunisia victory (13 May), prepared for Sicily invasion. Veteran of continuous combat since El Alamein (October 1942).

Supply Status: TRANSITIONAL - Q2 1943 saw XXX Corps in two distinct operational phases: (1) Early April: Wadi Akarit assault with adequate forward supply. Supply buildup from Gabes captured 29 March provided local base. (2) Mid-April onward: Corps reorganized after Tunisia victory 13 May.
