Supply & Logistics

Supply Status: Operation Crusader (18 Nov - 20 Dec 1941) placed extreme strain on XXX Corps logistics. Offensive launched from Egyptian railheads (Mersa Matruh) with 350km supply lines. British logistics advantage over Axis (Royal Navy control, proximity to Alexandria/Cairo bases) offset by rapid mobile operations. Tank fuel consumption critical constraint - Cruisers averaged 1.5 mpg. Water supply critical in desert (4-5L per man per day). Ammunition expenditure heavy during Sidi Rezegh battles (19-23 Nov). Supply columns vulnerable to Axis mobile forces during 'Dash to the Wire' (24-27 Nov). Field repair teams recovered ~70 damaged tanks during battle. By December supply lines stabilized after Tobruk relief.
