25th Infantry Division 'Bologna' (1942Q4)

39th Infantry Regiment 'Bologna'

  • Composition: Three battalions - catastrophically depleted from continuous combat since 1940. Home station Caserta. Many veterans killed/wounded, replacements inexperienced and poorly trained. Physical exhaustion from 400-mile march and continuous static defense.

40th Infantry Regiment 'Bologna'

  • Commander: Colonnello Francesco Baldini
  • Strength: 2,510 personnel
  • Composition: Three battalions - home station Naples. Regiment suffered cumulative losses from Gazala, First El Alamein, attrition. Morale declining sharply by October 1942 - awareness of strategic hopelessness, supply failures, British material superiority.

6th Bersaglieri Regiment

  • Commander: Colonnello Marco Tentori
  • Strength: 1,420 personnel
  • Composition: Two battalions - elite light infantry. Home station Bologna. Originally motorized but vehicles lost in retreat - now foot mobile only. Retains superior morale vs. line infantry but physically exhausted.

10th Artillery Regiment 'Volturno'

Supply & Logistics

Supply Status: Complete collapse. Division operating at catastrophic supply levels during Second Battle of El Alamein (October-November 1942). Water supply critically below survival minimum: 2.0L per day per man (vs. 3.5L minimum desert requirement) = 20,700L daily for 10,350 men. Primary Axis supply line catastrophically extended: 1,800km from Tripoli through Benghazi → Tobruk → Mersa Matruh → El Alamein. RAF air superiority causes 40-50% interdiction of coastal road supply convoys. Fuel reserves at 1.5 days - division completely immobile except local foot movements. Ammunition critically depleted: 2.5 days stocks, artillery rationed to 5 rounds per gun per day. Food supplies inadequate - troops on half rations, widespread malnutrition and disease (dysentery, malaria). Division had marched 400 miles from Gazala to El Alamein July 1942 lacking vehicles, never recovered from physical exhaustion. Italian logistics infrastructure in complete failure - division abandoned by strategic situation.
