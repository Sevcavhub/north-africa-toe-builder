North Africa Campaign 1940-1943: Complete Order of Battle

10ª Armata (1941Q1)

Command Structure

Commander: Generale d'Armata Generale d'Armata Giuseppe Tellera Parent Formation: Comando Superiore Forze Armate Africa Settentrionale (North Africa) Type: Unknown

Personnel

  • Total: 115,000 (5,750 officers, 17,250 NCOs, 92,000 enlisted)

Subordinate Units

XXI Corpo d'Armata

  • Commander: Generale di Corpo d'Armata Annibale Bergonzoli ('Electric Whiskers')
  • Composition: Primary defensive corps. Heavily engaged December 1940 - January 1941. Suffered catastrophic losses. Raggruppamento Maletti destroyed at Nibeiwa December 9, 1940. Bardia garrison fell January 5, 1941. Tobruk garrison captured January 22, 1941. Corps effectively destroyed by end January.

XXII Corpo d'Armata

  • Commander: Generale di Corpo d'Armata Petassi Mannella
  • Composition: Tobruk fortress garrison. Captured by British forces January 22, 1941 during Operation Compass. Approximately 25,000 POWs including corps commander.

XX Corpo d'Armata

  • Commander: Generale di Corpo d'Armata Giuseppe Cona
  • Composition: Mobile reserve corps. Retreated from Derna toward Benghazi. Destroyed at Battle of Beda Fomm February 5-7, 1941. Babini Armored Brigade (1st, 3rd M11 battalions, 21st, 60th L3 battalions, Bersaglieri) severely mauled and effectively destroyed.

1ª Divisione Libica

  • Strength: 7,200 personnel
  • Composition: Colonial division with Libyan troops. 1st and 2nd Raggruppamenti Lybian Infantry. Destroyed during Operation Compass.

2ª Divisione Libica

  • Strength: 7,400 personnel
  • Composition: Colonial division. Destroyed during Operation Compass December 1940 - January 1941.

4ª Divisione CC.NN. '3 Gennaio'

  • Strength: 7,800 personnel
  • Composition: Blackshirt (Fascist militia) division. Lower combat effectiveness than regular army divisions. Destroyed during Operation Compass.

Raggruppamento Maletti

  • Commander: Generale Pietro Maletti (killed December 9, 1940)
  • Strength: 2,500 personnel
  • Composition: DESTROYED December 9, 1940 at Battle of Nibeiwa. First engagement of Operation Compass. Commander Generale Pietro Maletti killed. Entire unit destroyed or captured. Listed for historical completeness only - not part of effective January 1941 strength.

63ª Divisione di Fanteria 'Cirene'

  • Strength: 12,800 personnel
  • Composition: Regular metropolitan infantry division. Heavy combat during Operation Compass. Significant casualties.

62ª Divisione di Fanteria 'Marmarica'

  • Strength: 12,300 personnel
  • Composition: Regular metropolitan infantry division. Destroyed during Operation Compass.

64ª Divisione di Fanteria 'Catanzaro'

  • Strength: 13,100 personnel
  • Composition: Regular metropolitan infantry division. Destroyed during Operation Compass.

61ª Divisione di Fanteria 'Sirte'

  • Strength: 11,800 personnel
  • Composition: Tobruk garrison division. Captured January 22, 1941. Approximately 25,000 POWs including garrison and fortress troops.

60ª Divisione di Fanteria 'Sabratha'

  • Strength: 12,600 personnel
  • Composition: Regular metropolitan infantry division. Destroyed at Beda Fomm February 5-7, 1941.

27ª Divisione Autotrasportabile 'Brescia'

  • Strength: 13,200 personnel
  • Composition: Motorized (autotrasportabile) division - truck-mobile infantry. Better mobility than standard infantry divisions. Destroyed at Beda Fomm February 5-7, 1941.

17ª Divisione di Fanteria 'Pavia'

  • Strength: 12,900 personnel
  • Composition: Regular metropolitan infantry division. Destroyed at Beda Fomm February 5-7, 1941.

Brigata Corazzata Speciale (Babini)

  • Commander: Generale Valentino Babini
  • Strength: 2,800 personnel
  • Composition: Armored reserve brigade. Severely mauled in battles around Mechili and Derna January 1941. Remnants destroyed at Beda Fomm February 5-7, 1941. Primary Italian armored force in Cyrenaica after Maletti Group destruction.
