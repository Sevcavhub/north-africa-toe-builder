Supply Status: Critical to catastrophic for Italian units, constrained to adequate for French units. First Italian Army defending southern and central Tunisia after 2000km retreat from El Alamein. Supply situation bifurcated: Italian X Corps facing catastrophic shortages (fuel 2-3 days, ammunition 30% establishment), while French XIX Corps somewhat better supplied through Allied logistics (fuel 4-6 days, ammunition rationed but adequate). Tunisian ports (Bizerte, Tunis, Sfax) under constant air attack. Water supply generally adequate from local sources (wells, wadis) but quality variable. Medical supplies critically short in Italian units, improving with Allied support for French units. Army dependent on sharing limited German supply priority and captured Allied stocks.
