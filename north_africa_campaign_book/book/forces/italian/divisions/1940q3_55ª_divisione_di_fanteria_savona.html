  • Composition: Divisional anti-tank company. 47mm guns effective against light armor only, inadequate against British Matilda II heavy infantry tanks (78mm frontal armor). Horse-drawn guns limited mobility. Significant vulnerability that would become apparent during British Operation Compass.

55ª Compagnia Genio

  • Composition: Division-level engineers. Responsible for field fortifications, mine warfare, and mobility support. Critical for preparing defensive positions in desert terrain. Limited equipment for extensive fortification work.

55ª Compagnia Trasmissioni

  • Composition: Division signals. Limited radio equipment - relied heavily on wire communications. Wire networks vulnerable to mobile warfare. Communications with higher headquarters and subordinate units challenging in mobile desert operations.

Battaglione Camicie Nere (attached)

  • Composition: MVSN (Fascist Militia) legion attached to division per March 1, 1940 directive. Provided additional infantry but lower combat effectiveness than regular infantry. Employed in secondary sectors and rear area security. No combat experience as of Q3 1940.

55ª Sezione Sanità

  • Composition: Division medical services. Limited evacuation capability due to lack of motorization. Serious casualties required evacuation to rear area hospitals via limited transport. Medical stocks adequate for defensive operations. Desert conditions created additional medical challenges (heat exhaustion, dysentery, water-borne diseases).

55ª Sezione Sussistenza

  • Composition: Division supply and logistics. Limited truck transport capacity constrained supply operations. Dependent on higher echelon transport for bulk supplies from ports. Water supply critical challenge in desert conditions. Food preservation difficult in extreme heat.

55° Plotone Carabinieri

  • Composition: Military police. Traffic control, prisoner handling, rear area security, prevention of looting, enforcement of military discipline.

55ª Banda Musicale
