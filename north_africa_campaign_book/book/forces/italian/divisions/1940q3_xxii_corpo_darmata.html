North Africa Campaign 1940-1943: Complete Order of Battle

XXII Corpo d'Armata (1940Q3)

Command Structure

Commander: Generale di Corpo d'Armata Enrico Pitassi Mannella Parent Formation: Unknown Type: Reserve Infantry Corps

Personnel

  • Total: 14,600 (755 officers, 2,185 NCOs, 11,660 enlisted)

Subordinate Units

61ª Divisione di Fanteria 'Sirte'

  • Composition: Only division subordinate to XXII Corps in Q3 1940. Metropolitan division formed May 1, 1938 for Libyan service. Named after ancient city of Sirte (Surt). Recruited from Calabria region (Catanzaro). Reserve role during September invasion. Later destroyed at Beda Fomm (February 7, 1941) during British pursuit after Operation Compass. 22° Reggimento Artiglieria di Corpo (XXII Corps Artillery Regiment)

  • Composition: Corps artillery numbered to match corps (22nd Regiment for XXII Corps). Provided medium/heavy artillery support beyond divisional 75/100mm capabilities. 105mm guns offered longer range and heavier punch for counter-battery, interdiction, and concentration fires. Motorized/tractor-drawn for mobility. Positioned in Tobruk area with firing positions covering approaches to fortress and potential deployment routes toward Bardia or Sidi Barrani. Artillery headquarters commanded by Generale di Brigata (brigadier general) as per Italian corps artillery doctrine.

22ª Compagnia Controcarri di Corpo (XXII Corps Anti-Tank Company)

  • Composition: Corps-level anti-tank reserve. 47mm guns effective against light armor and cruiser tanks but inadequate against British Matilda II heavy tanks (78mm frontal armor). Positioned to reinforce 61st Division anti-tank defense or cover critical approaches to Tobruk. Mobile deployment capability along coastal road network.

22ª Batteria Controaerei di Corpo (XXII Corps AA Battery)

  • Composition: Corps-level AA defense. 20mm Breda guns provided low-altitude point defense for corps headquarters and Tobruk port facilities. Limited effectiveness against RAF medium bombers but useful against low-level attack aircraft. Positioned around Tobruk harbor and supply depots. Supplemented by army-level AA units defending Tobruk fortress.

22ª Compagnia Genio di Corpo (XXII Corps Engineer Company)

  • Composition: Corps-level engineer reserve. Augmented 61st Division engineers for major tasks: Tobruk fortress improvement, minefield construction, demolition preparation, road/bridge maintenance along Via Balbia. Critical role in water supply infrastructure maintenance at Tobruk wells and desalination facilities. Prepared defensive obstacles in case of British counteroffensive from Mersa Matruh.

22ª Compagnia Trasmissioni di Corpo (XXII Corps Signal Company)

  • Composition: Corps-level communications. Maintained radio and wire communications between: (1) XXII Corps HQ and 10th Army HQ; (2) XXII Corps HQ and 61st Division HQ; (3) XXII Corps HQ and corps artillery regiment. Limited radio equipment typical of Italian forces - relied heavily on wire communications and motorcycle messengers. Positioned at Tobruk with wire network to subordinate units and lateral communications to XXI Corps at Bardia.

22° Plotone Ricognizione di Corpo (XXII Corps Reconnaissance Platoon)

  • Composition: Corps-level reconnaissance. AB 40/41 armored cars provided mobile reconnaissance capability for corps sector. Typical missions: route reconnaissance along Via Balbia toward Sidi Barrani or westward to Derna, liaison with adjacent corps (XXI at Bardia), security patrols around Tobruk perimeter, early warning of British movements from Mersa Matruh direction. Limited off-road capability in desert terrain - primarily road-bound.

22ª Sezione Sanità di Corpo (XXII Corps Medical Section)

  • Composition: Corps-level medical services. Coordinated casualty evacuation from 61st Division to Tobruk hospitals. Tobruk had excellent medical facilities including base hospital with surgical capability and sea evacuation to Benghazi or Tripoli. Corps medical section managed medical logistics and reinforced divisional medical units as required.

22ª Sezione Sussistenza di Corpo (XXII Corps Supply Section)

  • Composition: Corps-level logistics. Operated Tobruk supply base including port reception, warehousing, and forward distribution to 61st Division and corps troops. Managed ammunition dumps, fuel storage, ration distribution, and equipment maintenance. Tobruk port provided sea supply line from Tripoli (approximately 900km west) and Benghazi (400km west). Critical node in 10th Army logistics chain supporting Egyptian invasion. Reserve corps status allowed stockpiling and preparation for future operations.
