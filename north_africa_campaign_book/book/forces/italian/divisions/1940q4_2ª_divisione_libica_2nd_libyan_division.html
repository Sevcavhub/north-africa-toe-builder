Subordinate Units

3° Raggruppamento di Fanteria Libica (3rd Libyan Infantry Group)

  • Composition: Libyan colonial infantry. Battalion-sized formation equivalent to regiment in metropolitan divisions. Indigenous soldiers with Italian officers. Part of division defensive position at Tummar East December 1940.

4° Raggruppamento di Fanteria Libica (4th Libyan Infantry Group)

  • Composition: Libyan colonial infantry. Battalion-sized formation equivalent to regiment in metropolitan divisions. Indigenous soldiers with Italian officers. Part of division defensive position at Tummar East December 1940.

9° Battaglione Carri L (9th Tank Battalion 'L')

  • Composition: Light tank battalion equipped with obsolete L3 tankettes. Unique to 2nd Libyan Division. L3 tankettes (6-13mm armor, 2x 8mm MGs) effective only for reconnaissance. Vulnerable to all British anti-tank weapons. Mechanically unreliable in desert heat. Provided symbolic armor capability rather than genuine combat power.

2° Raggruppamento di Artiglieria Libica (2nd Libyan Artillery Group)

  • Composition: Mixed artillery group. 77mm guns from WWI Austrian stocks. 105mm howitzers for indirect fire. 65mm infantry guns for close support. 75mm battalion attached from CCNN 28 Ottobre division for additional firepower. One 65mm battery attached from G.A.F. (Guardia alla Frontiera) frontier guard troops.

Battaglione Genio (Engineer Battalion)

  • Composition: Divisional engineers. Limited mechanization. Responsible for field fortifications, water point development, road maintenance. Constructed fortified camps at Tummar East.

Compagnie Anticarro (Anti-Tank Companies)

  • Composition: Division-level heavy weapons. Two AT companies with 47mm/32 guns. One company attached from G.A.F. (Guardia alla Frontiera) frontier guard troops. Inadequate for defense against British armor.

Servizi Divisionali (Divisional Services)

  • Composition: Division support units. Heavy reliance on camel transport columns. Limited motorized transport. Medical facilities basic. Supply situation critical Q4 1940 due to extended lines.
