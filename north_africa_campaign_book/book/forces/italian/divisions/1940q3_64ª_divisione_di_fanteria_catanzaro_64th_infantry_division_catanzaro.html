Subordinate Units

141° Reggimento Fanteria 'Catanzaro' (141st Infantry Regiment 'Catanzaro')

  • Composition: Landed at Derna June 3, 1940. Three fusilier battalions + Support Weapons Company (65/17 guns) + Mortar Company (81mm). Regiment completely green, formed from six different regiments in mainland Italy.

142° Reggimento Fanteria 'Catanzaro' (142st Infantry Regiment 'Catanzaro')

  • Composition: Landed at Tobruk June 6, 1940. Three fusilier battalions + Support Weapons Company (65/17 guns) + Mortar Company (81mm). Regiment completely green, inadequate training.

203° Reggimento Artiglieria 'Catanzaro' (203rd Artillery Regiment 'Catanzaro')

  • Composition: Artillery regiment previously part of disbanded 3rd CC.NN. Division '21 Aprile' (May 1940). Three artillery groups with WWI-era guns. Limited mobility.

LXIV Battaglione Mitraglieri (LXIV Machine Gun Battalion)

  • Composition: Heavy weapons battalion for defensive positions. Four MMG companies.

LXIV Battaglione Genio Misto (LXIV Mixed Engineer Battalion)

  • Composition: Divisional engineers. Field fortifications, mine warfare, water points, road maintenance. Limited mechanization.

XX Battaglione Carri 'L' (XX Tank Battalion 'L')

  • Composition: Attached tank battalion. L3 tankettes obsolete, only effective for reconnaissance. Vulnerable to British anti-tank guns.

64ª Compagnia Anticarro (64th Anti-tank Company)

  • Composition: Division anti-tank company. 47mm guns inadequate against British heavy armor.

Servizi Divisionali (Divisional Services)

  • Composition: Medical, supply, transport, signals, maintenance, military police. Oversized due to division's logistics problems. Carabinieri sections for military police. Service units struggled with vehicle maintenance crisis.
