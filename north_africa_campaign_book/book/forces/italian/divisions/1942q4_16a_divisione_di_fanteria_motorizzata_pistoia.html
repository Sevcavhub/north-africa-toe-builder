Supply & Logistics

Supply Status: Strained to critical. Division arrived in Tunisia August 1942 (Q3) via sea transport from Italy through Sicily. Supply route: Italy → Sicily → Tunisia ports (Tunis, Bizerte, Sfax). Allied naval and air interdiction increasingly effective in Q4 1942, reducing supply flow significantly. Competition with German units and other Italian divisions for limited supplies. Fuel shortages limit motorized mobility. Ammunition adequate for defensive operations but insufficient for sustained offensive action. Food and medical supplies adequate in Q4 but deteriorating.
