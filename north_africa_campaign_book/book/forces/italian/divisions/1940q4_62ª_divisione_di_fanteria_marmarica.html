62ª Divisione di Fanteria 'Marmarica' (1940Q4)

Personnel

Subordinate Units

115° Reggimento Fanteria 'Marmarica'

  • Composition: Binary organization regiment. Each battalion: 4 rifle companies + 1 machine gun company + 1 mortar platoon. Regimental weapons: 65mm infantry guns, 81mm mortars. Regiment suffered casualties at Tummar (9-11 December), approximately 200 men lost (killed, wounded, captured during withdrawal). Occupied Bardia southern sector positions in late Q4. Personnel strength reduced from Q3 levels (2,620 to 2,550).

116° Reggimento Fanteria 'Marmarica'

  • Composition: Binary organization regiment. Each battalion: 4 rifle companies + 1 machine gun company + 1 mortar platoon. Regimental weapons: 65mm infantry guns, 81mm mortars. Regiment suffered casualties at Tummar (9-11 December), approximately 200 men lost during withdrawal. Occupied Bardia Ponticelli sector positions in late Q4. Personnel strength reduced from Q3 levels (2,620 to 2,550).

44° Reggimento Artiglieria 'Marmarica'

  • Composition: Reduced artillery establishment (24 guns vs standard 36). Horse-drawn artillery struggled during Tummar withdrawal - 2 guns abandoned due to vehicle breakdowns and British pressure. Regiment at 22 operational guns by late Q4. Ammunition stocks critical (~120 rounds/gun). Artillery positioned within Bardia perimeter to support infantry defensive positions. Personnel reduced from Q3 (1,185 to 1,150) due to casualties and sickness.

62ª Compagnia Controcarri

  • Composition: Divisional anti-tank company. 47mm guns proven inadequate against British Matilda tanks at Tummar (armor up to 78mm frontal). Critical weakness exposed during Operation Compass. Guns positioned at key approaches in Bardia perimeter but insufficient to stop concentrated British armor assault. Personnel reduced slightly from Q3 (118 to 115) due to casualties at Tummar.

44ª Compagnia Genio

  • Composition: Division engineers. Constructed Bardia defensive fortifications throughout Q4 1940: wire obstacles, minefields, concrete strongpoints, trench systems. Engineers worked continuously to strengthen Ponticelli sector positions. Also conducted demolitions during Tummar withdrawal (destroyed abandoned equipment). Mine warfare capability used extensively - laid defensive minefields around Bardia perimeter. Personnel reduced from Q3 (182 to 175) due to casualties.

62ª Compagnia Trasmissioni

  • Composition: Division signals. Established wire communications network within Bardia fortress. Radio communications to XXI Corps headquarters and adjacent units (1st/2nd CC.NN. divisions). Communications equipment losses during Tummar withdrawal (radio sets destroyed under air attack). Heavy reliance on wire and messengers within Bardia perimeter. Personnel reduced from Q3 (138 to 135).

LXXIV Battaglione Camicie Nere 'Marmarica'

  • Composition: MVSN Blackshirt battalion attached to division. Lower combat effectiveness than regular infantry but provided additional manpower for defensive positions. Employed in secondary sectors at Bardia. Battalion suffered casualties at Tummar (~20 men lost). Morale declining due to defeats. Personnel reduced from Q3 (652 to 630).

62ª Sezione Sanità

  • Composition: Division medical services. Treated casualties from Tummar fighting withdrawal (approximately 400 wounded). Established aid stations within Bardia fortress. Medical supplies adequate in early Q4 but declining by December. Serious casualties evacuated to Bardia fortress hospital (shared with garrison). Increasing cases of respiratory illness in winter. Personnel reduced from Q3 (218 to 210).

62ª Sezione Sussistenza

  • Composition: Division supply and logistics. Managed supply dumps at Bardia fortress. Supply situation increasingly difficult: fuel scarce, ammunition reduced, rations adequate but monotonous. British RAF attacks and naval interdiction reduced seaborne supply to Bardia. Supply trucks reduced due to losses at Tummar (~15 vehicles). Division dependent on Bardia fortress stockpiles. Personnel reduced from Q3 (278 to 270).

62° Plotone Carabinieri

  • Composition: Military police. Traffic control, rear area security, prevention of desertion. Desertion increasing in Q4 as defeats mounted - Carabinieri enforced discipline. Limited prisoner handling during Tummar withdrawal (minimal time for British prisoner evacuation). Primarily security duties in Bardia fortress. Personnel reduced from Q3 (44 to 42).

62ª Banda Musicale

  • Composition: Ceremonial and morale purposes. Continued performing in Bardia fortress to maintain morale despite deteriorating situation. Band performances less frequent in Q4 due to operational pressures and declining morale. Personnel reduced from Q3 (35 to 33).
