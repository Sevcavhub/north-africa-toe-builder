Deutsches Afrikakorps (DAK)

  • Commander: Multiple (Generalleutnant Heinz Ziegler, others)
  • Strength: 25,875 personnel
  • Composition: Transferred from Panzerarmee Afrika to 5. Panzerarmee operational control. Veteran formations, severely depleted after El Alamein. Split between northern sector (21. Panzer) and southern sector (15. Panzer, 90. leichte under Italian First Army). Total 136 tanks.

10. Panzer-Division

  • Commander: Generalmajor Fritz Freiherr von Broich
  • Strength: 12,800 personnel
  • Composition: Arrived from France December 1942. Experienced from Eastern Front but lacking desert experience. Commanded by von Broich, tank regiment by future Field Marshal von Manteuffel, operations officer (Ia) Oberst Stauffenberg (later attempted Hitler assassination July 20, 1944). 70 tanks including 1 Tiger from sPzAbt 501. Participated in Kasserine Pass and Sidi Bou Zid victories.

Hermann Göring Division (Kampfgruppe Schmid)

  • Commander: Generalmajor Joseph Schmid
  • Strength: 9,000 personnel
  • Composition: Partial deployment only - Panzer-Regiment still forming in France. Approximately 9,000 personnel deployed as Kampfgruppe Schmid (two grenadier regiments, flak regiment, partial artillery). No tanks in theater. Fought as motorized infantry with heavy flak support (88mm guns). Division destroyed Tunisia May 1943; France-based elements became nucleus for Sicily reconstitution.

Supply & Logistics

Supply Status: CRITICAL - Army operating under severe constraints. Allied naval/air interdiction of Mediterranean convoys prevents adequate resupply from Sicily/Italy. Tunisia ports (Tunis, Bizerte, Sfax) operational but under constant Allied air attack. Axis shipping losses catastrophic: January-March 1943 approximately 40-50% of supply ships/aircraft lost in transit. Fuel situation most critical constraint - limits mobile operations to defensive counterattacks only. Ammunition adequate for defensive combat but insufficient for sustained offensive. No heavy equipment replacement capability - every tank/vehicle loss permanent. Personnel reinforcements limited - approximately 5,000-8,000 replacements arrived Q1 vs 12,000-15,000 casualties. Strategic supply situation hopeless - Tunisia bridgehead unsustainable without Mediterranean control.
