Supply & Logistics

Supply Status: Critically strained during July-September 1942. Mediterranean shipping losses severe - only 17,000 tons received in August vs 500,000 tons to British Eighth Army. Chronic fuel shortages constraining all mobile operations. DAK had only 3 days fuel for sustained combat during Battle of Alam Halfa (30 Aug - 5 Sept). Ammunition adequate but declining. Reliance on captured British vehicles and supplies critical for continued operations. Water rationing at 2.5-3 liters per man per day.
