5. leichte Division (1941Q2)

Command Structure

Commander: Generalmajor Generalmajor Johann von Ravenstein Parent Formation: Deutsches Afrikakorps (DAK) Type: Light Motorized Division

Personnel

  • Total: 12,000 (450 officers, 1,800 NCOs, 9,750 enlisted)

Subordinate Units

Panzer-Regiment 5

  • Commander: Oberst Gerhard Müller (acting, then Oberst Olbrich)
  • Strength: 1,680 personnel
  • Composition: From 3. Panzer-Division. Initial Africa deployment Feb 1941: 25× Pz.I, 45× Pz.II, 75× Pz.III, 20× Pz.IV. By Q2 reinforced with newer Pz.III Ausf G.

Schützen-Regiment 200

  • Commander: Unknown
  • Strength: 2,850 personnel
  • Composition: Unique 'regiment' structure: actually 2 separate MG battalions without standard rifle companies. Reflects improvised leichte division organization.

Aufklärungs-Abteilung 3

  • Commander: Unknown
  • Strength: 580 personnel
  • Composition: From 3. Panzer-Division. Critical for desert reconnaissance and screening. One zug used Volkswagen Kübelwagens.

I./Artillerie-Regiment 75

  • Commander: Unknown
  • Strength: 420 personnel
  • Composition: From 3. Panzer-Division. Only field artillery organic to 5. leichte Division (significantly lighter than standard panzer division with 3 battalions). Additional artillery support from corps assets.

Panzerjäger-Abteilung 39

  • Commander: Unknown
  • Strength: 385 personnel
  • Composition: Towed AT guns. 3.7cm 'door-knocker' increasingly inadequate vs British Matilda II. 5cm PaK 38 in limited supply Q2 1941.

Panzerjäger-Abteilung 605 (gepanzert)

  • Commander: Unknown
  • Strength: 320 personnel
  • Composition: Self-propelled variant using captured Czech 47mm AT guns. Mobile AT capability. Better penetration than 3.7cm.

Flak-Bataillon 606 (Selbstfahrlafette)

  • Commander: Unknown
  • Strength: 380 personnel
  • Composition: Mobile AA defense for advancing formations. Critical against RAF fighter-bomber attacks.

I./Flak-Regiment 33

  • Commander: Unknown
  • Strength: 440 personnel
  • Composition: From 3. Panzer-Division. 88mm guns proved devastatingly effective in anti-tank role against British cruiser tanks. Rommel's preferred anti-tank weapon.

200th z.b.V. Pionier-Bataillon

  • Commander: Unknown
  • Strength: 485 personnel
  • Composition: z.b.V. (zur besonderen Verwendung) = for special employment. Mine warfare and fortification critical for Tobruk siege.

Nachrichten-Abteilung 39

  • Commander: Unknown
  • Strength: 345 personnel
  • Composition: Communications critical for mobile desert operations and coordination with DAK HQ.

Divisions-Nachschubführer

  • Commander: Unknown
  • Strength: 2,140 personnel
  • Composition: Extensive logistical tail essential for desert operations 900km from Tripoli. Water and fuel supply paramount. Medical facilities for casualties and desert illnesses.
