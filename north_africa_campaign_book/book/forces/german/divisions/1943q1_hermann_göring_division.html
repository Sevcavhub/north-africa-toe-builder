Hermann Göring Division (1943Q1)

Command Structure

Commander: Generalmajor Generalmajor Joseph Schmid Parent Formation: 5. Panzerarmee (Tunisia) Type: panzer_division

Personnel

  • Total: 9,000 (350 officers, 1,500 NCOs, 7,150 enlisted)

Subordinate Units

Grenadier-Regiment 1 Hermann Göring

  • Composition: Deployed to Tunisia. Two battalions operational. III. Battalion staff became I./Panzer-Regiment HG (remained in France). Regiment at 70% strength.

Grenadier-Regiment 2 Hermann Göring

  • Composition: Formed from Jäger-Regiment Hermann Göring (ex-Fallschirmjäger-Regiment 5). Partially deployed to Tunisia. Elements still forming. At 60% strength.

Panzer-Regiment Hermann Göring

  • Composition: NOT DEPLOYED. Regiment forming in Mont-de-Marsan, France during Tunisia campaign. No tanks arrived in Tunisia with Kampfgruppe Schmid.

Flak-Regiment General Göring (I. & II. Abteilung)

  • Composition: Two battalions (I. & II.) deployed to Tunisia. Mixed 88mm heavy and 37mm/20mm light flak. Regiment destroyed in Tunisia May 1943. III. Abteilung remained in training in Europe.

Artillerie-Regiment Hermann Göring (Partial)

  • Composition: Elements deployed, not full regiment. Primarily one battalion with 105mm howitzers. Regiment still forming in France.

Panzer-Aufklärungs-Abteilung Hermann Göring (Elements)

  • Composition: Partial deployment. Motorcycle and armored car companies. Kradschützen company destroyed in Tunisia May 1943.

Panzer-Pionier-Bataillon Hermann Göring (Elements)

  • Composition: Engineer elements deployed for defensive fortification work. Full battalion not present.

Panzer-Nachrichten-Abteilung Hermann Göring (Elements)

  • Composition: Partial signals capability. Radio and wire communications.

Division Supply & Medical Services (Partial)

  • Composition: Minimal supply train. Medical services limited. Reliant on Luftwaffe logistics support.

Feldersatz-Bataillon Hermann Göring (Elements)

  • Composition: Field replacement elements. Limited training capability in Tunisia.

Supply & Logistics

Supply Status: Severely constrained. Division deployed piecemeal via airlift and sea transport to Tunisia. Supply lines from Sicily increasingly interdicted by Allied air and naval forces. Fuel extremely limited due to shipping losses. Ammunition adequate for defensive operations only. No heavy equipment replacement capability. Units operating at 60% authorized strength due to partial deployment.

  • Operational Radius: Limited to 80-120km from supply depots due to fuel shortages. Division elements tied to coastal areas and road networks. No offensive operational capability beyond local counterattacks.
