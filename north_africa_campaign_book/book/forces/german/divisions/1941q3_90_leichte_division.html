Infanterie-Regiment 361 (Afrika-Regiment)

  • Commander: Oberstleutnant Hans-Lewin von Barby
  • Strength: 2,400 personnel
  • Composition: Unique unit: composed largely of former French Foreign Legion volunteers (anti-communist Germans/Austrians who served in Légion Étrangère). Tough, experienced troops but poorly equipped initially.

Supply & Logistics

Supply Status: Strained - Weak motorization (only 1,200 trucks vs 1,500+ for panzer divisions). Extended supply lines from Tripoli (~950 km) via coastal road. Division hastily assembled from disparate units with limited organic transport. Heavily reliant on captured British vehicles and equipment. Supply base: Tripoli with forward dumps at Derna, Tmimi, and Gazala. Water critical constraint at 10L/man/day minimum.
