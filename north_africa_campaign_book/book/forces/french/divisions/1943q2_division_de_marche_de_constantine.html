Division de Marche de Constantine (1943Q2)

Command Structure

Commander: Général de Brigade Général de Brigade Joseph Welvert Parent Formation: French XIX Corps Type: Colonial Infantry Division (Constantine)

Personnel

  • Total: 11,950 (495 officers, 1,790 NCOs, 9,665 enlisted)

Subordinate Units

2e Régiment de Tirailleurs Algériens (2nd RTA)

  • Strength: 2,580 personnel
  • Composition: Reduced from Q1 casualties (estimated 350 killed/wounded). Received 230 replacements April 1943 from Constantine depots. Algerian indigenous troops from Constantine region with French officers and NCOs. Maintained good cohesion throughout final offensive.

4e Régiment de Tirailleurs Algériens (4th RTA)

  • Strength: 2,520 personnel
  • Composition: Reduced from Q1 casualties (estimated 380 killed/wounded). Received 250 replacements April 1943. Algerian indigenous troops from Constantine region with French officers and NCOs. Heavy combat in April breakthrough operations.

8e Régiment de Tirailleurs Algériens (8th RTA)

  • Strength: 2,470 personnel
  • Composition: Heaviest Q1 casualties (estimated 420 killed/wounded). Received 300 replacements April 1943, largest of three regiments. Algerian indigenous troops from Constantine region with French officers and NCOs. Spearhead unit during May advance on Tunis.

Groupement de Reconnaissance Divisionnaire de Constantine (GRDC)

  • Strength: 770 personnel
  • Composition: Reduced from combat losses and mechanical attrition. Critical role in pursuit operations during Axis collapse, May 7-13.

Régiment d'Artillerie de Constantine (RAC)

  • Strength: 1,020 personnel
  • Composition: Mix of World War I vintage and interwar French artillery. Ammunition resupply excellent during final offensive - no restrictions on fire missions. Provided critical support during breakthrough operations.

Bataillon de Chars (Tank Battalion)

  • Strength: 370 personnel
  • Composition: Reduced from Q1 losses (2 tanks total). No replacements received. M3 Stuarts performed well with US logistical support. Somua S35s increasingly maintenance-challenged but effective when operational.

Bataillon du Génie (Engineer Battalion)

  • Strength: 620 personnel
  • Composition: Critical role in clearing extensive Axis minefields during advance. Heavy workload clearing defensive belts around Tunis perimeter. Bridging requirements minimal in dry season.

Services Divisionnaires (Divisional Services)

  • Strength: 880 personnel
  • Composition: Medical company overwhelmed during final offensive (high casualties April-May). Signals company maintained good communications throughout mobile operations. Transport company benefited from US lend-lease truck deliveries. Veterinary services maintained for mule transport in mountain units.
