North Africa Campaign 1940-1943: Complete Order of Battle

Forces Organization Structure

This section presents the hierarchical military organization of forces in North Africa (1940-1943), organized by combatant nation and organizational echelon.

Organization

Hierarchy: Theater → Army → Corps → Division → Brigade/Regiment

Structure:

  • Organized by nation (British/Commonwealth, German, Italian, American, French)
  • Then by echelon level (armies, corps, divisions)
  • Files named by quarter + unit (e.g., 1942q2_xxx_corps.md)

Nations Covered

British/Commonwealth (154 unit-quarters)

Includes British, Indian, Australian, New Zealand, South African, and Polish forces operating under British command.

German (59 unit-quarters)

Wehrmacht forces: Panzerarmee Afrika, Deutsches Afrikakorps, and subordinate divisions.

Italian (147 unit-quarters)

Regio Esercito forces: Tenth Army, multiple corps, and colonial divisions.

American (23 unit-quarters)

US II Corps and subordinate divisions (Operation Torch onwards, 1942-Q4 through 1943).

French (19 unit-quarters)

Free French forces operating under British command, including colonial divisions.
