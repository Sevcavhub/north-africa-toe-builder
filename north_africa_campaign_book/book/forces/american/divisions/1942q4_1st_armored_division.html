Supply & Logistics

Supply Status: Strained but adequate for initial operations. Primary constraints: long supply lines from Oran (800km to Tunisia front), limited port capacity at Oran, inadequate forward storage facilities, and inexperienced logistics staff. Fuel and ammunition prioritized over spare parts and maintenance supplies.
