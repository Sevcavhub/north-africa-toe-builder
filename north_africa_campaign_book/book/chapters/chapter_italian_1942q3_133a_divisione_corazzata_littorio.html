11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

Doctrinal Role

Mobile armored exploitation and breakthrough operations in desert warfare. Combined arms coordination of tanks, motorized infantry, and self-propelled artillery.

Special Capabilities

  • Desert mobility with tracked and wheeled vehicles
  • Self-propelled artillery organic to division
  • Integrated anti-tank and anti-aircraft defense
  • Motorized Bersaglieri for combined arms operations

Tactical Innovations

  • 75/18 self-propelled guns on M13/40 chassis for close support
  • 90/53 dual-purpose AA/AT guns for air defense and anti-armor
  • Armored cavalry reconnaissance group with light tanks and armored cars

Known Issues and Limitations

  • Tank mechanical reliability poor in desert conditions
  • M13/40 and M14/41 outclassed by British Crusader and American Grant tanks
  • Fuel and water supply chronically inadequate
  • Radio equipment insufficient for coordinated operations
  • Spare parts shortage reducing operational readiness
  • Desert heat causing high vehicle attrition

Desert Adaptations

Increased water tanker allocation, dust filters for engines (often inadequate), extended fuel capacity modifications, desert camouflage schemes, increased medical support for heat casualties
