11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

Doctrinal Role

Mobile armored warfare, breakthrough operations, counter-attack force

Special Capabilities

  • Fast-moving armored formation
  • Combined arms coordination with Bersaglieri infantry
  • Mobile artillery support
  • Desert warfare operations

Tactical Innovations

  • Integration of self-propelled AT guns (90/53 on M15 chassis)
  • Use of armored cars for wide reconnaissance
  • Mobile artillery concept with motorized batteries
  • LANCIERI DI NOVARA cavalry tradition in armored reconnaissance

Known Issues and Limitations

  • M13/40 tanks inferior to British Crusaders and American Grants
  • Chronic fuel and water shortages in desert
  • Thin armor on M13/40 vulnerable to 2-pdr AT guns
  • Mechanical reliability issues in desert conditions
  • Limited tank recovery and repair capabilities
  • 47mm AT gun inadequate against heavier Allied armor

Desert Adaptations

Extensive desert modifications including air filters, sun shields, increased water and fuel storage. Water tankers critical for operations. White-washed vehicles for desert camouflage.
