11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

Doctrinal Role

Auto-transportable infantry division - partial motorization for improved mobility in desert operations. Designed for rapid deployment and mobile defense.

Special Capabilities

  • Partial motorization (auto-transportable) - improved mobility vs. standard infantry divisions
  • Organic tank battalion (VI Tank Battalion with 37x M13/40)
  • Greater reconnaissance capacity with armored cars

Combined Arms Integration

[MANUAL: Add section about infantry-tank-artillery cooperation, air support, engineer support]

Known Issues and Limitations

  • Obsolete WWI-era artillery (75/27 Mod 1906 from 1906)
  • Inadequate anti-tank weapons (only 12x 47/32 guns)
  • Minimal anti-aircraft defense (12 guns total)
  • M13/40 tanks inferior to British Matildas and Cruisers
  • Insufficient training for mobile desert warfare
  • Logistics infrastructure unable to sustain extended operations far from base

Desert Adaptations

Limited desert modifications. Division struggled with supply constraints, fuel shortages, and mechanical reliability of vehicles in desert conditions. Water supply inadequate for prolonged operations.
