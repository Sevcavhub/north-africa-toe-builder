11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

Doctrinal Role

Division NOT operational as cohesive unit during 1942-Q2. Brigades dispersed to separate theaters for garrison/training duties. GOC Tuker strongly opposed dispersal. 11th Brigade saw combat at Tobruk (captured). 5th Brigade rushed to First Alamein July 1942. Division not reunited until October 1942.

  • Mountain warfare expertise (utilized later in Italy)
