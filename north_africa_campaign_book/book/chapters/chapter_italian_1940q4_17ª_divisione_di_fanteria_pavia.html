11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

Doctrinal Role

Reserve division in XX Corps, Tripolitania sector. Coastal defense and internal security duties during Q4 1940. Did NOT participate in Italian invasion of Egypt (September 1940) or Operation Compass defensive battles (December 1940-February 1941). Held in strategic reserve west of Tripoli.

Special Capabilities

  • Semi-motorized (autotrasportabile) for road movement along Via Balbia
  • Desert-adapted with specialized vehicles (SPA AS.37 Sahariano)
  • Binary division structure (2 infantry regiments vs 3 in ternary divisions)
  • L3/35 tankette battalion provides limited mobile reconnaissance and infantry support
