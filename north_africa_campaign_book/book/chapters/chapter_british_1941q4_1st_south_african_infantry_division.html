11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

Doctrinal Role

Commonwealth infantry division organized on British model. Primarily infantry assault role with limited mobility. Brigades deployed for both offensive and defensive operations during Operation Crusader.

Special Capabilities

  • Experienced in desert warfare from East African Campaign
  • Good integration with British 8th Army command structure
  • Locally-produced Marmon-Herrington armored cars for reconnaissance

Tactical Innovations

  • Use of South African-built Marmon-Herrington armored cars adapted for desert conditions
  • Combined-arms coordination developed during East African operations

Combined Arms Integration

[MANUAL: Add section about infantry-tank-artillery cooperation, air support, engineer support]

Known Issues and Limitations

  • Limited anti-tank capability - 2-pounders insufficient against German armor
  • Forced to use 25-pounders in direct-fire anti-tank role with heavy losses
  • 5th SA Brigade nearly destroyed at Sidi Rezegh on 23 November 1941
  • Long supply lines from Egypt caused fuel and ammunition shortages
  • Vehicle breakdown rate high due to desert conditions and sand abrasion

Desert Adaptations

Division transitioned from East African terrain to Western Desert. Vehicles modified with sand filters and extra water capacity. Personnel trained in desert navigation and water discipline.
