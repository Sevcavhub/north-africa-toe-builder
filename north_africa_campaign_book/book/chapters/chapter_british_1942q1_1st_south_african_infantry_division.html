8. Supply and Logistics

Supply Status

Improved from Q4 1941. Division established in defensive positions along northern sector of Gazala Line with static supply depots. Primary supply route from Tobruk (35km) and Benghazi (250km). Water supply adequate from coastal wells and desalination. Division was 5,570 men under-strength as of 8 January 1942 (17,617 vs authorized 23,187). Supply situation stable during defensive preparations but constrained by limited port capacity at Tobruk and long haul from Egypt.

11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

Doctrinal Role

Commonwealth infantry division organized on British model. Primary role in Q1 1942: defensive operations along northern sector of Gazala Line from the coast southward. Preparing for anticipated Axis offensive while recovering from Operation Crusader losses. Division responsible for coastal defenses down to junction with 4th Indian Division.

Special Capabilities

  • Experienced in desert warfare from East African Campaign and Operation Crusader
  • Strong integration with British 8th Army command structure
  • Locally-produced Marmon-Herrington armored cars for reconnaissance
  • Defensive positions extensively prepared with minefields and wire obstacles

Tactical Innovations

  • Use of South African-built Marmon-Herrington armored cars adapted for desert conditions
  • Combined-arms coordination developed during East African operations
  • Defensive box system along Gazala Line with interlocking fields of fire

Combined Arms Integration

Known Issues and Limitations

  • Under-strength by 5,570 personnel (76% of authorized strength)
  • Limited anti-tank capability - 2-pounders insufficient against upgraded German armor
  • Replacement of 5th SA Brigade losses from Sidi Rezegh still incomplete
  • Defensive positions constrain mobility and offensive capabilities
  • Integration of Polish Carpathian Brigade created language and coordination challenges

Desert Adaptations

Division fully adapted to Western Desert conditions by Q1 1942. Vehicles equipped with sand filters and extra water capacity. Personnel trained in desert navigation, water discipline, and defensive position construction. Experience from Operation Crusader integrated into defensive tactics.
