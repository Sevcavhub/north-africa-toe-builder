11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

Doctrinal Role

Semi-motorized infantry division for mobile operations in North Africa

Special Capabilities

  • Truck-borne mobility (when transport available from corps level)
  • Organic armoured battalion for infantry support
  • Enhanced reconnaissance capability with dedicated armoured car battalion

Combined Arms Integration

[MANUAL: Add section about infantry-tank-artillery cooperation, air support, engineer support]

Known Issues and Limitations

  • Insufficient organic motor transport - requires corps-level allocation for full mobility
  • Limited anti-tank capability with only 47/32mm guns
  • Inadequate radio communications equipment
  • Water supply challenges in desert operations

Desert Adaptations

Division sent to Libya February 1941, beginning adaptation to desert warfare conditions including revised water discipline and vehicle maintenance procedures
