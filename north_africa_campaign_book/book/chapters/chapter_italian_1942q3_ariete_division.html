North Africa Campaign 1940-1943: Complete Order of Battle

132ª Divisione Corazzata "Ariete"

Italian Armored Division, 1942-Q3 (July-September 1942)

1. Overview

The third quarter of 1942 witnessed Ariete Division fighting defensive battles at El Alamein under Generale di Divisione Francesco Antonio Arena with 6,800 personnel and tanks ~90-100. Participating in First Battle of El Alamein (July 1-27) and Battle of Alam Halfa (August 31-September 7), the division demonstrated continued Italian tactical competence despite overwhelming British material superiority. Arena's command maintained division cohesion under extreme pressure.

Key Statistics (1942-Q3):

  • Personnel: 6,800
  • Tanks: ~90-100 (M13/40 and some M14/41)
  • Commander: Generale di Divisione Francesco Antonio Arena
  • Operations: First Alamein, Alam Halfa
  • Strategic Result: Strategic defensive

2. First Alamein and Alam Halfa

At First Alamein (July), Ariete held southern sector, repulsing British probes. M13/40 tanks, though obsolescent, proved adequate in defensive role with hull-down positioning. At Alam Halfa (August 31-September 7), division participated in Rommel's final offensive, suffering losses to British Sherman tanks and concentrated artillery. RAF air superiority inflicted heavy casualties on Italian logistics.

3. Material Inferiority Worsens

By Q3 1942, British fielded 300+ Sherman tanks with 75mm guns, overwhelming Italian M13/40 capabilities (47mm gun, 42mm armor). Ariete's value lay in providing additional armored mass and freeing German tanks for offensive operations, but combat effectiveness declining relative to Allied improvements.

4. Historical Significance

Q3 1942 marked Axis high-water mark before Second Alamein catastrophe. Ariete fought skillfully in defensive role but material disadvantages insurmountable. Division positioned for final battles (Second Alamein, October-November) where it would be destroyed.
