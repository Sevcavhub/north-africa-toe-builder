11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

Doctrinal Role

Static defense of Bardia fortress perimeter. CC.NN. divisions were primarily organized for internal security and occupation duties. Division held the central 'Ponticelli' sector of Bardia defenses alongside elements of 62nd Infantry Division 'Marmarica'.

Special Capabilities

  • Political reliability (Fascist Party militia)
  • Fortified position defense
  • Internal security operations

  • Lower combat effectiveness compared to regular Army divisions
  • Inferior equipment allocation
  • Limited training for modern warfare
  • Weak anti-tank capability against British Matilda tanks
  • Insufficient motorized transport
  • Poor coordination with regular Army units
  • Surrounded and isolated from December 1, 1940

Desert Adaptations

Minimal. CC.NN. divisions lacked specialized desert equipment and training. Static defensive positions at Bardia provided some protection but limited tactical flexibility.
