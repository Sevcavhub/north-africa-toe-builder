11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

Doctrinal Role

Infantry division for static defense of Buqbuq sector guarding Sidi Barrani coastal approaches October-December 1940. Autotrasportabile classification meant truck-transportable but division lacked adequate operational vehicles (only 33% by Q4). Green division unsuited for mobile operations, assigned to fortified defensive positions.

Special Capabilities

  • Standard Italian infantry division structure (two regiments)
  • Full complement of support weapons (when operational)
  • Larger personnel strength than colonial/CCNN divisions (10,500 men)
  • Some combat experience gained in static defense Q4 (vs completely green in Q3)
