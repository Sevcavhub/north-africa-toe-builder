# Strategic Situation - 1941-Q2

## North Africa Theater

**Siege of Tobruk**
**Dates**: April 10 - December 10, 1941 (242 days)
**Combatants**: British, Australian, Polish, Czech vs German, Italian

## Historical Context

Australian and British garrison holds Tobruk port under siege. Critical to deny Rommel the port and tie down Axis forces. Supplied by Royal Navy destroyers ('Tobruk Ferry Service'). Rommel launches multiple assaults, all repelled. Siege lifted during Operation Crusader (Nov-Dec 1941).

## Key Forces

### Allied Forces
- 9th Australian Division
- 70th British Division
- 32nd Army Tank Brigade


**German Forces**: 5th Light Division, 15th Panzer Division (arrived May), Afrika Korps

### Axis Forces

**Italian Forces**:
- Bologna Division
- Pavia Division

**German Forces**:
- 5th Light Division
- 15th Panzer Division (arrived May)
- Afrika Korps

## Key Locations and Objectives

- Tobruk fortress
- El Duda
- Sidi Rezegh (breakout battles)

## Outcome

Successful defense, siege lifted Nov-Dec 1941
