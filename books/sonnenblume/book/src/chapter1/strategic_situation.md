# Strategic Situation - 1941-Q1

## North Africa Theater

**Operation Sonnenblume**
**Dates**: February 14 - March 31, 1941
**Combatants**: British, Commonwealth vs German, Italian

## Historical Context

German deployment to North Africa following Italian collapse in Operation Compass. Rommel arrives Feb 12, 1941 with 5th Light Division. Immediately launches offensive, recapturing Cyrenaica and pushing British back to Egyptian frontier. Establishes siege of Tobruk (Apr 10).

## Key Forces

### Allied Forces
- 2nd Armoured Division
- 9th Australian Division


**German Forces**: 5th Light Division, Rommel arrives Feb 12

### Axis Forces

**Italian Forces**:
- Ariete Division
- Brescia Division

**German Forces**:
- 5th Light Division
- Rommel arrives Feb 12

## Key Locations and Objectives

- El Agheila
- Benghazi
- Tobruk (besieged)
- Halfaya Pass

## Outcome

Axis success, British retreat to Egypt, Tobruk besieged

## Historical Notes

15th Panzer Division arrives May-June 1941, NOT during Sonnenblume
