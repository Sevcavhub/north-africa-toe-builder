🎖️ North Africa Campaign Books

1940-1943: The Desert War

Professional wargaming scenario books covering the complete North Africa Campaign. Each book contains detailed equipment datacards, historical scenarios, tactical guides, and orders of battle for BattleGroup tabletop wargaming.

About This Project

Publication-ready scenario books for the BattleGroup WWII tabletop wargaming system, meticulously researched and professionally formatted. Each book represents months of historical research, database development, and content generation.

Built from a comprehensive database of 469 equipment items, 612 AFV specifications, and 402 unit-quarter extractions covering every major formation that fought in North Africa from 1940-1943.

Each book includes:

📊

Equipment Datacards

V5.5 format with nation colors, multi-row armament tables, special rules, and silhouette images for tanks, guns, and vehicles

⚔️

Historical Scenarios

Combined arms scenarios with tactical objectives, force compositions, and historical context for each battle

🗺️

Orders of Battle

Complete organizational hierarchies from Corps to Company level showing unit structure and equipment

📚

Historical Sources

Comprehensive citations and bibliography documenting the historical research behind each battle
