# Strategic Situation - 1942-Q3

## North Africa Theater

**First Battle of El Alamein**
**Dates**: July 1-27, 1942
**Combatants**: British, Commonwealth, South African, Indian, New Zealand vs German, Italian

## Historical Context

Critical defensive battle. Auchinleck halts Rommel just 60 miles from Alexandria. El Alamein position between sea and Qattara Depression prevents flanking. Series of attritional attacks exhaust both sides. High-water mark of Axis expansion. Rommel's logistics overstretched. British prevent breakthrough to Nile Delta.
