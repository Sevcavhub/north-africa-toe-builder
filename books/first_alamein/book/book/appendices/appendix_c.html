First Battle of El Alamein

Appendix C: Historical Sources

This appendix documents the primary and secondary sources used to construct the First El Alamein scenarios, orders of battle, and equipment datacards. First El Alamein (July 1942) was Auchinleck's defensive masterpiece that halted Rommel's offensive at the last defensible position before the Nile Delta, featuring Commonwealth diversity (Australian, New Zealand, South African, Indian forces) and desperate defensive battles.

Gaps and Limitations

  1. Indian division equipment details: Some gaps in British Army Lists for Indian formations
  2. Italian forces exact strengths: Depleted states estimated from secondary sources
  3. 23rd Armoured Brigade final casualties: Exact El Mreir losses disputed (80-87 tanks)
  4. 164. leichte Division records: Newly arrived division, limited documentation

  • British/Commonwealth forces: 88-92% (excellent official histories, war diaries)
  • Australian forces: 92-95% (excellent AWM archives)

  • South African forces: 85-90% (good SA official history)

  • German forces: 85-90% (good Tessin/Nafziger, depleted states estimated)
