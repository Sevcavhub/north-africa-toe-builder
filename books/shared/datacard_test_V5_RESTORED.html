✅ V5 Restored Format - November 10, 2025

  • ✅ Source: Git commit 0e632052 (before November 10)
  • ✅ Grid layout: 3 columns (repeat(3, 1fr))
  • ✅ Title alignment: Right-aligned
  • ✅ Two-table structure: Vehicle Stats + Weapon Performance
  • ✅ Table 1: VEHICLE | MOVEMENT | ARMOUR | ARMAMENT
  • ✅ Table 2: WEAPON | AMMO | HE | RANGE (6 bands)
  • ✅ Nation colors: German, British, Italian, American themes
  • ✅ Special rules: Displayed in header when present
  • ✅ Multi-weapon: Secondary weapons on additional rows
