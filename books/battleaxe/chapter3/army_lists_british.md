## Force Selection Rules

### Battle Rating
- Calculate your force's Battle Rating (BR) by adding the BR of all units
- Typical game size: 400-600 points, BR 30-40

### Core Units
- Must include at least one HQ unit
- Must include at least two infantry or tank units

### Support Units
- Artillery, anti-tank guns, and support weapons
- Limited to 40% of total points

### Special Rules
- **British Resolve:** British units have +1 morale in defensive positions
- **Desert Adapted:** All Commonwealth forces are considered acclimatized to desert conditions
- **Combined Arms:** May mix infantry and tank units in same platoon
