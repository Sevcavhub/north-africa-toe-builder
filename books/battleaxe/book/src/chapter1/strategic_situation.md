# Strategic Situation - 1941-Q2

## North Africa Theater

**Operation Battleaxe**
**Dates**: June 15-17, 1941
**Combatants**: British, Commonwealth vs German, Italian

## Historical Context

British offensive to relieve Tobruk garrison using 'Tiger Convoy' tank reinforcements (240 tanks). Failed due to German 88mm anti-tank screen at Halfaya Pass ('Hellfire Pass'). Demonstrated effectiveness of integrated German combined-arms tactics. Churchill's first major setback in desert.

## Key Forces

### Allied Forces
- 7th Armoured Division
- 4th Indian Division
- 4th Armoured Brigade


**German Forces**: 15th Panzer Division, 5th Light Division, Afrika Korps

### Axis Forces

**Italian Forces**:
- Ariete Division
- Bologna Division

**German Forces**:
- 15th Panzer Division
- 5th Light Division
- Afrika Korps

## Key Locations and Objectives

- Halfaya Pass
- Fort Capuzzo
- Sollum
- Point 206

## Outcome

German defensive victory, British retreat
