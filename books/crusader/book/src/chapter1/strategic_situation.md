# Strategic Situation - 1941-Q4

## North Africa Theater

**Operation Crusader**
**Dates**: November 18 - December 30, 1941
**Combatants**: British, Commonwealth, South African, New Zealand vs German, Italian

## Historical Context

Largest British offensive to date. Relieved Tobruk siege (242 days) and pushed Rommel back to El Agheila. Confused tank battles around Sidi Rezegh. Rommel's 'dash to the wire' failed. British logistical superiority prevailed despite tactical setbacks. Temporary British success.
