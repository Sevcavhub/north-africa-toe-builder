# Strategic Situation - 1942-Q4

## North Africa Theater

**Operation Torch**
**Dates**: November 8-16, 1942
**Combatants**: American, British vs Vichy French, German (reinforcements), Italian

## Historical Context

Allied amphibious landings in Morocco and Algeria. American combat debut in North Africa. Vichy French resistance ends after 3 days. Opens second front, trapping Axis between Montgomery (east) and Eisenhower (west). Germans rush reinforcements to Tunisia. Race for Tunis begins. Strategic turning point - Axis now fighting defensive campaign on two fronts.
