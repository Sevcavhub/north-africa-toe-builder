Western Desert Force

Commander: Lieutenant-General Lieutenant-General Richard Nugent O'Connor

Personnel Strength

  • Total: 38,050
  • Officers: 2,310
  • NCOs: 7,485
  • Enlisted: 28,255

Tanks

Total: 243

Medium Tanks

  • Matilda II Infantry Tank: 48 total (40 operational) - 7th RTR - Corps troops. 47 tanks participated at Nibeiwa (9 Dec). 22 operational by 27 December after combat losses and mechanical breakdowns. One penetrated at Nibeiwa, several lost to mines.
  • Cruiser Mk IV (A13): 65 total (58 operational) - 7th Armoured Division - 2nd RTR in 4th Armoured Brigade
  • Cruiser Mk I (A9): 25 total (21 operational) - 7th Armoured Division - Mixed across 1st, 6th, 7th Hussars
  • Cruiser Mk II (A10): 20 total (17 operational) - 7th Armoured Division - Mixed across 1st RTR, 8th Hussars

Light Tanks

  • Light Tank Mk VI: 85 total (69 operational) - 7th Armoured Division - 3rd Hussars and reconnaissance units. Mechanical wear issues in desert. Used only for reconnaissance, inadequate for combat.

Armoured Cars

Total: 172

  • Morris CS9 Armoured Car: 84 total (78 operational)
  • Rolls-Royce Armoured Car: 59 total (53 operational) - 11th Hussars primary equipment in 7th Armoured Division
  • Marmon-Herrington Mk I: 29 total (26 operational)

Artillery

Total Guns: 218

Field Artillery

  • QF 25-pounder Field Gun: 120 total - Primary field artillery for Operation Compass. Total confirmed as 120 guns for WDF at start of operation.

Anti-Tank Guns

  • Ordnance QF 2-pounder: 72 total - Standard British anti-tank gun 1940. Effective against Italian M11/39 and L3 tankettes.

Anti-Aircraft Guns

  • Bofors 40mm Gun: 12 total - 7th Armoured Division: 12 guns (1st Light AA Battery joined 4 January 1941, but allocated to force in late December)
