# Strategic Situation - 1940-Q4

## North Africa Theater

**Operation Compass**
**Dates**: December 9, 1940 - February 7, 1941
**Combatants**: British, Commonwealth vs Italian

## Historical Context

British counteroffensive against Italian invasion of Egypt. Italy invaded Sept 1940, Britain counterattacked Dec 1940. Devastating British victory capturing 130,000 prisoners and destroying 10 Italian divisions. Germans NOT yet in North Africa.

## Key Forces

### Allied Forces
- Western Desert Force
- 7th Armoured Division
- 4th Indian Division


**No German forces present in this battle** - Germans did not arrive in North Africa until Operation Sonnenblume (February 1941).

### Axis Forces

**Italian Forces**:
- 10th Army
- Maletti Group
- 1st Libyan Division
- 2nd Libyan Division

## Key Locations and Objectives

- Sidi Barrani
- Bardia
- Tobruk
- Beda Fomm

## Outcome

Decisive British victory, Italy requests German aid
