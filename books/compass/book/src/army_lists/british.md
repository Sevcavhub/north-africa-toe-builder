## Western Desert Force

**Commander**: Lieutenant-General Lieutenant-General Richard Nugent O'Connor

### Personnel Strength

- **Total**: 38,050
- **Officers**: 2,310
- **NCOs**: 7,485
- **Enlisted**: 28,255

### Tanks

**Total**: 243

#### Medium Tanks

- **Matilda II Infantry Tank**: 48 total (40 operational) - _7th RTR - Corps troops. 47 tanks participated at Nibeiwa (9 Dec). 22 operational by 27 December after combat losses and mechanical breakdowns. One penetrated at Nibeiwa, several lost to mines._
- **Cruiser Mk IV (A13)**: 65 total (58 operational) - _7th Armoured Division - 2nd RTR in 4th Armoured Brigade_
- **Cruiser Mk I (A9)**: 25 total (21 operational) - _7th Armoured Division - Mixed across 1st, 6th, 7th Hussars_
- **Cruiser Mk II (A10)**: 20 total (17 operational) - _7th Armoured Division - Mixed across 1st RTR, 8th Hussars_

#### Light Tanks

- **Light Tank Mk VI**: 85 total (69 operational) - _7th Armoured Division - 3rd Hussars and reconnaissance units. Mechanical wear issues in desert. Used only for reconnaissance, inadequate for combat._

### Armoured Cars

**Total**: 172

- **Morris CS9 Armoured Car**: 84 total (78 operational)
- **Rolls-Royce Armoured Car**: 59 total (53 operational) - _11th Hussars primary equipment in 7th Armoured Division_
- **Marmon-Herrington Mk I**: 29 total (26 operational)

### Artillery

**Total Guns**: 218

#### Field Artillery

- **QF 25-pounder Field Gun**: 120 total - _Primary field artillery for Operation Compass. Total confirmed as 120 guns for WDF at start of operation._

#### Anti-Tank Guns

- **Ordnance QF 2-pounder**: 72 total - _Standard British anti-tank gun 1940. Effective against Italian M11/39 and L3 tankettes._

#### Anti-Aircraft Guns

- **Bofors 40mm Gun**: 12 total - _7th Armoured Division: 12 guns (1st Light AA Battery joined 4 January 1941, but allocated to force in late December)_

### Infantry Weapons (Primary)

- **Lee-Enfield No.1 Mk III Rifle**: 27,200 (Bolt Action Rifle)
- **Bren Gun Light Machine Gun**: 1,180 (Light Machine Gun)
- **Vickers .303 Machine Gun**: 208 (Heavy Machine Gun)
