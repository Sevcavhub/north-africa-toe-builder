# Appendix A: Quick Reference - Operation Compass

This appendix provides essential tables and references for quick gameplay during Operation Compass scenarios (December 1940 - February 1941).

## BattleGroup Core Rules Summary

### Turn Sequence

1. **Orders Phase**: Both players draw battle rating (BR) chits if needed, issue orders
2. **Aircraft Phase**: Resolve air strikes, air-to-air combat
3. **Initiative Phase**: Roll for initiative (higher roll chooses who activates first)
4. **Action Phase**: Alternate unit activations until both players pass
5. **Rally Phase**: Attempt to rally pinned units, repair breakdowns
6. **End Phase**: Remove transient markers, check victory conditions

### Key Mechanics

**Morale/Battle Rating**: Each force has BR (typically 30-60 points for battalion-level games). When you take a BR chit (from losses, objectives lost, etc.), draw randomly from your pool. Game ends when a side's BR is reduced to 0 or below.

**Orders/Command**: Most units need orders to act aggressively. Roll 2D6 vs command rating. Artillery always needs orders. Units within command range of HQ don't need to test for defensive actions.

**Movement Rates**: Infantry 5" per action, motorized 10", fast vehicles 12-16", slow tanks 8-10"

**Shooting**: Roll to hit (modified by range, cover, movement), then target rolls armor save (if armored) or cover save (if infantry/soft target). Penetrating hits on vehicles roll on damage table.

**Pinning**: Units taking fire that causes casualties become pinned. Pinned units have reduced effectiveness and must rally before moving or firing effectively.

## Special Rules Alphabetical Index

### A-C

**APCR Ammunition**: Limited tungsten-core rounds. +2 penetration up to 30". German 50mm PaK 38 and late Panzer III only. Max 3 shots per game.

**Breakdown - Crusader**: Roll D6 when moving at full speed. On 1, immobilized. -1 modifier to all breakdown rolls.

**Breakdown - General**: D6 roll when moving at max speed over rough terrain. On 1, vehicle breaks down for D3 turns (1-4) or permanently (5-6).

**Breakdown - M13/40**: Roll at start of game. On 1-2, reduce movement by -4" all game (sand filters). -1 to breakdown rolls.

**Concealed 88mm**: 88mm guns may start concealed. Not revealed until first shot. Represents hull-down positions in "Hellfire Pass" scenario.

### D-H

**Dawn Attack**: First 2 turns have limited visibility (reduce spotting range to 18"). Represents early morning assaults (Scenario 1, 5).

**Desert Terrain**: Open terrain provides minimal cover. Most areas are open ground (no cover save) unless specifically marked.

**Dual Purpose (25-pdr)**: May fire HE (indirect artillery) or AP (direct AT). Switching modes requires 1 full action.

**Escarpment**: Impassable except at marked passes. Units on top get +6" spotting range. Reverse slope provides hard cover.

**Fortified Positions**: Infantry in fortifications get +2 cover save. AT guns in prepared positions count as hull-down (+1 armor save).

**Hull-Down**: Tanks in hull-down positions get +1 armor save vs frontal shots. 88mm guns at "Hellfire Pass" use this rule.

### I-R

**Italian Morale**: Some Italian units have lower morale ratings. Use scenario-specific morale values (typically -1 vs German/British equivalents).

**Matilda Thick Armor**: Immune to guns under 50mm except at very close range. +2 armor save vs 37mm and 47mm guns.

**Mechanically Unreliable**: See Breakdown rules above.

**Minefield**: Moving through minefields requires engineering support. Unsupported units roll D6: 1-2 = hit mine (vehicle immobilized/destroyed, infantry take casualties).

**Night Action**: Visibility limited to 12". Close combat bonuses apply. Units may attempt to infiltrate. Used in Scenario 26 (Bir Hacheim breakout).

**No HE - 2-pounder**: British 2-pdr guns cannot fire HE. May only engage armored targets effectively (vs infantry, counts as MG fire).

**Portee Mounting**: AT guns mounted on trucks. Move at truck speed but must dismount (1 action) to fire. Vulnerable when mounted (truck armor).

**Pursuit**: German forces in pursuit scenarios get +2" movement when chasing retreating enemy.

### S-Z

**Smoke Rounds**: Create 6" diameter smoke screen, blocks LOS. Lasts 2 turns. Limited supply (max 2 per gun per game).

**Stuka Dive Bomber**: +1 to hit stationary/slow targets. Siren causes morale check (-1) to units within 12" of target.

**Truck Vulnerability**: Trucks have no armor save. Any hit that strikes automatically damages the vehicle.

**Veteran Crews (German)**: German tank crews get +1 to shooting when firing first in an engagement. Represents superior training.

**Wadi Crossing**: Only tracked vehicles may cross wadis. Roll D6: on 1-2, vehicle stuck and immobilized.

**Withdrawal Option**: In some scenarios, forces may voluntarily withdraw off table edge to preserve BR. Must exit designated edge.

| Situation | Save Modifier |
|-----------|---------------|

| **Side shot** | -1 to armor save |
| **Rear shot** | -2 to armor save |

### Morale Checks

| Situation | Target Number |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Standard morale check** | 2D6 ≤ unit morale rating |
| **Took casualties this turn** | +1 to roll (harder) |
| **HQ within command range** | -1 to roll (easier) |
| **Elite/Veteran unit** | -1 to roll |
| **Reduced strength (<50%)** | +1 to roll |
| **Stuka siren nearby** | +1 to roll |

### Vehicle Damage Table (D6)

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| **1** | Crew stunned - no permanent damage, pinned |
| **2-3** | Damaged - mobility or weapon hit, roll again for specific |
| **4-5** | Knocked out - vehicle immobilized, crew may bail out |
| **6** | Destroyed - catastrophic kill, vehicle explodes, crew casualties |

**Modifiers to damage roll**:
- Penetrated by 88mm: +1
- Hit in side/rear: +1
- Riveted construction (M13/40): +1 to crew casualty
- Hit by APCR: +1

## Force Organization Quick Reference

### British Force Structure (December 1940 - February 1941)

**Battalion-level game** (typical BR 35-45):
- 1x HQ (command value 8-9)
- 2-3x Infantry platoons (30-35 men each)
- 1x Support platoon (mortars, MGs, carriers)
- 1x Tank squadron (8-12 tanks)
- 1x Artillery battery (4-6 guns, typically 25-pdr)
- 1x AT gun section (2-4 guns, typically 2-pdr)

### German Force Structure (December 1940 - February 1941)

**Battalion-level game** (typical BR 35-45):
- 1x HQ (command value 9-10, veteran)
- 2-3x Panzergrenadier platoons (30-35 men each)
- 1x Support platoon (mortars, MG34s, PAK)
- 1x Panzer company (10-15 tanks)
- 1x Artillery battery (4-6 guns, 105mm or 88mm)
- 1x AT gun section (2-4 guns, PAK 38 or 88mm)

### Italian Force Structure (December 1940 - February 1941)

**Battalion-level game** (typical BR 30-40):
- 1x HQ (command value 7-8)
- 3-4x Infantry platoons (30-35 men each, larger companies)
- 1x Support platoon (mortars, Breda HMGs)
- 1x Tank company (8-12 M13/40 or L3/35)
- 1x Artillery battery (4-6 guns, 75mm)
- 1x AT gun section (4-6 guns, 47mm common)

## Combat Example: Matilda II vs 88mm at Halfaya Pass

**Situation**: British Matilda II (78mm frontal armor) advances on concealed German 88mm position at 25" range.

**Turn 1**: Matilda moves 6" cross-country, now at 19" range. 88mm not yet revealed (concealed).

**Turn 2**: Matilda moves another 6", now at 13" range. German player reveals 88mm and fires.

**To Hit Roll**:
- Base: 4+
- Range (under 30"): +0
- Target in open: +0
- **Result: Needs 4+ to hit**

German rolls 5 - **HIT**

**Penetration**:
- 88mm penetration @ 13": Automatic vs 78mm armor
- Matilda armor save: Base 4+ (thick armor)
- 88mm modifier: -1 (powerful gun)
- **Final save needed: 5+**

British rolls 3 - **PENETRATED**

**Damage Roll**:
- Base D6 roll: 4
- 88mm bonus: +1
- **Final: 5 = Knocked Out**

**Result**: Matilda II knocked out, crew bails out, British player draws BR chit.

**Historical Note**: This was the typical outcome at "Hellfire Pass" - 11 Matilda IIs destroyed in minutes by concealed 88mm guns.
