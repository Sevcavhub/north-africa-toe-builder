5. Panzerarmee (Fifth Panzer Army)

Commander: Generaloberst Generaloberst Hans-Jürgen von Arnim

Personnel Strength

  • Total: 89,600
  • Officers: 3,420
  • NCOs: 15,800
  • Enlisted: 70,380

Tanks

Total: 206

Heavy Tanks

  • Pz.Kpfw.VI Tiger Ausf E: 1 total - Single Tiger from schwere Panzer-Abteilung 501 attached to 10. Panzer-Division as III./Panzer-Regiment 7. Participated in Kasserine Pass offensive with devastating effect.

Medium Tanks

  • Pz.Kpfw.III Ausf L/M (5cm KwK 39 L/60): 34 total (27 operational) - DAK divisions (15. Panzer: 26, 21. Panzer: 8 estimated). Upgunned Panzer III with long 50mm gun.
  • Pz.Kpfw.III Ausf N (7.5cm KwK L/24): 21 total (17 operational) - DAK 21. Panzer-Division. Infantry support variant with short 75mm howitzer.
  • Pz.Kpfw.IV Ausf G (7.5cm KwK 40 L/43): 32 total (26 operational) - DAK divisions (15. Panzer: 19, 21. Panzer: 13 estimated). Earlier long-barreled Panzer IV production.
  • Pz.Kpfw.IV Ausf H (7.5cm KwK 40 L/48): 49 total (42 operational) - DAK 21. Panzer-Division (49 tanks). Newest production with improved armor and slightly longer gun.
  • Pz.Kpfw.III (various) - 10. Panzer: 28 total (22 operational) - 10. Panzer-Division light tanks (Ausf J/L variants estimated)
  • Pz.Kpfw.IV (various) - 10. Panzer: 13 total (11 operational) - 10. Panzer-Division medium tanks (mix of G/H variants)

Light Tanks

  • Pz.Kpfw.II Ausf F: 28 total (19 operational) - 10. Panzer-Division reconnaissance elements. Largely obsolete by 1943, used for scouting only.

Armoured Cars

Total: 99

  • Sd.Kfz. 222 (2cm KwK 30): 51 total (39 operational) - Light armored car. DAK: 27, 10. Panzer: 12, Hermann Göring: 12
  • Sd.Kfz. 231 (8-Rad): 26 total (20 operational) - Heavy armored car (8-wheeled). DAK: 12, 10. Panzer: 8, Hermann Göring: 6
  • Sd.Kfz. 232 (8-Rad): 20 total (15 operational) - Heavy armored car with radio. DAK: 15, 10. Panzer: 5
  • Sd.Kfz. 234 Puma: 2 total - New 8-wheeled armored car with 5cm gun. DAK only - very limited numbers in theater.

Artillery

Total Guns: 459

Field Artillery

  • 10.5cm leFH 18: 88 total - Standard German light field howitzer. DAK: 46, 10. Panzer: 24, Hermann Göring: 18
  • 15cm sFH 18: 39 total - Heavy field howitzer. DAK: 21, 10. Panzer: 12, Hermann Göring: 6
  • 10cm K 18: 8 total - Heavy field gun. 10. Panzer-Division only.
  • 15cm sIG 33: 4 total - Heavy infantry gun. 10. Panzer-Division.

Anti-Tank Guns

  • 7.5cm PaK 40: 44 total - Primary German anti-tank gun 1943. DAK: 24, 10. Panzer: 12, Hermann Göring: 8
  • 5cm PaK 38: 45 total - Earlier anti-tank gun, still effective vs medium armor. DAK: 33, 10. Panzer: 8, Hermann Göring: 4
  • Marder II/III SPG: 12 total - Self-propelled anti-tank guns. DAK: 8 (Marder tank destroyers), 10. Panzer: 4 (Marder III with captured Soviet 7.62cm gun)

Anti-Aircraft Guns

  • 8.8cm FlaK 18/36/37: 30 total - Famous 88mm dual-role AA/AT gun. DAK: 18, 10. Panzer: 4, Hermann Göring: 8. Critical for anti-tank defense.
  • 3.7cm FlaK 36/37: 15 total - Medium flak. DAK: 9, Hermann Göring: 6
  • 2cm FlaK 38: 38 total - Light automatic flak. DAK: 28, 10. Panzer: 6, Hermann Göring: 4
  • 2cm Flakvierling 38: 2 total - Quad-mounted 20mm flak. 10. Panzer-Division. Devastating anti-aircraft/ground support weapon.

Infantry Weapons (Primary)

Panzerarmee Afrika (Heeresgruppe Afrika)

Personnel Strength

  • Total: 9,000
  • Officers: 3,100
  • NCOs: 14,200
  • Enlisted: 60,700

Tanks

Total: 240

Heavy Tanks

  • Pz.Kpfw.VI Tiger Ausf E: 18 total (15 operational) - schwere Panzer-Abteilung 501 (15 Tigers operational). First heavy tanks in North Africa. Deployed Feb 1943, achieved tactical impact at Kasserine Pass despite small numbers.

Medium Tanks

  • Pz.Kpfw.III Ausf L/M (5cm L/60): 82 total (65 operational) - Remaining serviceable Panzer IIIs consolidated from 10., 15., 21. Panzer-Divisionen
  • Pz.Kpfw.III Ausf N (7.5cm L/24): 28 total (22 operational) - Infantry support variant with short howitzer
  • Pz.Kpfw.IV Ausf F2/G (7.5cm L/43): 42 total (32 operational) - Older long-barreled variants
  • Pz.Kpfw.IV Ausf H (7.5cm L/48): 40 total (34 operational) - New production with improved gun and armor. Arrived Q1 1943 as reinforcement.

Light Tanks

  • Pz.Kpfw.II Ausf F: 30 total (20 operational) - Reconnaissance role only. Largely obsolete by 1943.

Armoured Cars

Total: 68

  • SdKfz 222: 35 total (26 operational)
  • SdKfz 232 (8-rad): 22 total (16 operational)
  • SdKfz 234 Puma: 11 total (9 operational) - First Pumas deployed to North Africa

Artillery

Total Guns: 380

Field Artillery

  • 10.5cm leFH 18: 98 total (88 operational) - Standard divisional field howitzer
  • 15cm sFH 18: 32 total (28 operational)
  • 10cm K 18: 15 total (13 operational) - Heavy field guns

Anti-Tank Guns

  • 5cm PaK 38: 72 total (65 operational) - Still standard but increasingly inadequate vs new Allied armor
  • 7.5cm PaK 40: 52 total (48 operational) - Primary anti-tank gun. Effective against all Allied tanks.
  • 3.7cm PaK 36: 16 total (14 operational) - Obsolete. Retained only due to shortages.

Anti-Aircraft Guns

  • 8.8cm FlaK 18/36/37: 42 total (38 operational) - Dual-role anti-aircraft and anti-tank. Critical asset. Reduced from 48 in 1942-Q3 due to losses.
  • 2cm FlaK 30/38: 53 total (45 operational) - Light AA defense inadequate vs Allied air superiority
