# 7. Withdrawal Under Fire
## SITUATION REPORT
**Date**: June 17, 1941, 08:00
**Location**: East of Capuzzo, withdrawing toward Egypt

British 7th Armoured Division conducts fighting withdrawal as Battle of Mareth Line fails. German forces pursue, attempting to inflict maximum casualties before British reach safety.

Part of Battle of Mareth Line operation.
