# Strategic Situation - 1943-Q1

## North Africa Theater

**Battle of Mareth Line**
**Dates**: March 16-28, 1943
**Combatants**: British, Commonwealth, New Zealand, Free French vs German, Italian

## Historical Context

Breaking the last major Axis defensive line. Old French fortifications (Maginot-style). Montgomery's frontal assault (XXX Corps) stalls. Freyberg's New Zealand Corps 'left hook' via Tebaga Gap succeeds. Axis retreat to Wadi Akarit. Part of final Tunisia campaign. Rommel had already left Africa (Mar 9). Von Arnim in command.
