**Commander**: Generaloberst Generaloberst Hans-Jürgen von Arnim

### Tanks

**Total**: 206

#### Heavy Tanks

- **Pz.Kpfw.VI Tiger Ausf E**: 1 total - _Single Tiger from schwere Panzer-Abteilung 501 attached to 10. Panzer-Division as III./Panzer-Regiment 7. Participated in Kasserine Pass offensive with devastating effect._

#### Medium Tanks

- **Pz.Kpfw.III Ausf L/M (5cm KwK 39 L/60)**: 34 total (27 operational) - _DAK divisions (15. Panzer: 26, 21. Panzer: 8 estimated). Upgunned Panzer III with long 50mm gun._
- **Pz.Kpfw.III Ausf N (7.5cm KwK L/24)**: 21 total (17 operational) - _DAK 21. Panzer-Division. Infantry support variant with short 75mm howitzer._
- **Pz.Kpfw.IV Ausf G (7.5cm KwK 40 L/43)**: 32 total (26 operational) - _DAK divisions (15. Panzer: 19, 21. Panzer: 13 estimated). Earlier long-barreled Panzer IV production._
- **Pz.Kpfw.IV Ausf H (7.5cm KwK 40 L/48)**: 49 total (42 operational) - _DAK 21. Panzer-Division (49 tanks). Newest production with improved armor and slightly longer gun._
- **Pz.Kpfw.III (various) - 10. Panzer**: 28 total (22 operational) - _10. Panzer-Division light tanks (Ausf J/L variants estimated)_
- **Pz.Kpfw.IV (various) - 10. Panzer**: 13 total (11 operational) - _10. Panzer-Division medium tanks (mix of G/H variants)_

#### Light Tanks

- **Pz.Kpfw.II Ausf F**: 28 total (19 operational) - _10. Panzer-Division reconnaissance elements. Largely obsolete by 1943, used for scouting only._

### Armoured Cars

**Total**: 99

- **Sd.Kfz. 222 (2cm KwK 30)**: 51 total (39 operational) - _Light armored car. DAK: 27, 10. Panzer: 12, Hermann Göring: 12_

- **Sd.Kfz. 232 (8-Rad)**: 20 total (15 operational) - _Heavy armored car with radio. DAK: 15, 10. Panzer: 5_
- **Sd.Kfz. 234 Puma**: 2 total - _New 8-wheeled armored car with 5cm gun. DAK only - very limited numbers in theater._

**Total Guns**: 459

- **10.5cm leFH 18**: 88 total - _Standard German light field howitzer. DAK: 46, 10. Panzer: 24, Hermann Göring: 18_

- **10cm K 18**: 8 total - _Heavy field gun. 10. Panzer-Division only._
- **15cm sIG 33**: 4 total - _Heavy infantry gun. 10. Panzer-Division._

#### Anti-Tank Guns

- **7.5cm PaK 40**: 44 total - _Primary German anti-tank gun 1943. DAK: 24, 10. Panzer: 12, Hermann Göring: 8_
- **5cm PaK 38**: 45 total - _Earlier anti-tank gun, still effective vs medium armor. DAK: 33, 10. Panzer: 8, Hermann Göring: 4_
- **Marder II/III SPG**: 12 total - _Self-propelled anti-tank guns. DAK: 8 (Marder tank destroyers), 10. Panzer: 4 (Marder III with captured Soviet 7.62cm gun)_

#### Anti-Aircraft Guns

- **8.8cm FlaK 18/36/37**: 30 total - _Famous 88mm dual-role AA/AT gun. DAK: 18, 10. Panzer: 4, Hermann Göring: 8. Critical for anti-tank defense._
- **3.7cm FlaK 36/37**: 15 total - _Medium flak. DAK: 9, Hermann Göring: 6_
- **2cm FlaK 38**: 38 total - _Light automatic flak. DAK: 28, 10. Panzer: 6, Hermann Göring: 4_
- **2cm Flakvierling 38**: 2 total - _Quad-mounted 20mm flak. 10. Panzer-Division. Devastating anti-aircraft/ground support weapon._

## Panzerarmee Afrika (Heeresgruppe Afrika)

### Personnel Strength

- **Total**: 9,000
- **Officers**: 3,100
- **NCOs**: 14,200
- **Enlisted**: 60,700

### Tanks

**Total**: 240

#### Heavy Tanks

- **Pz.Kpfw.VI Tiger Ausf E**: 18 total (15 operational) - _schwere Panzer-Abteilung 501 (15 Tigers operational). First heavy tanks in North Africa. Deployed Feb 1943, achieved tactical impact at Kasserine Pass despite small numbers._

#### Medium Tanks

- **Pz.Kpfw.III Ausf L/M (5cm L/60)**: 82 total (65 operational) - _Remaining serviceable Panzer IIIs consolidated from 10., 15., 21. Panzer-Divisionen_
- **Pz.Kpfw.III Ausf N (7.5cm L/24)**: 28 total (22 operational) - _Infantry support variant with short howitzer_
- **Pz.Kpfw.IV Ausf F2/G (7.5cm L/43)**: 42 total (32 operational) - _Older long-barreled variants_
- **Pz.Kpfw.IV Ausf H (7.5cm L/48)**: 40 total (34 operational) - _New production with improved gun and armor. Arrived Q1 1943 as reinforcement._

#### Light Tanks

- **Pz.Kpfw.II Ausf F**: 30 total (20 operational) - _Reconnaissance role only. Largely obsolete by 1943._

### Armoured Cars

**Total**: 68

- **SdKfz 222**: 35 total (26 operational)
- **SdKfz 232 (8-rad)**: 22 total (16 operational)
- **SdKfz 234 Puma**: 11 total (9 operational) - _First Pumas deployed to North Africa_

### Artillery

**Total Guns**: 380

#### Field Artillery

- **10.5cm leFH 18**: 98 total (88 operational) - _Standard divisional field howitzer_
- **15cm sFH 18**: 32 total (28 operational)
- **10cm K 18**: 15 total (13 operational) - _Heavy field guns_

#### Anti-Tank Guns

- **5cm PaK 38**: 72 total (65 operational) - _Still standard but increasingly inadequate vs new Allied armor_
- **7.5cm PaK 40**: 52 total (48 operational) - _Primary anti-tank gun. Effective against all Allied tanks._
- **3.7cm PaK 36**: 16 total (14 operational) - _Obsolete. Retained only due to shortages._

- **8.8cm FlaK 18/36/37**: 42 total (38 operational) - _Dual-role anti-aircraft and anti-tank. Critical asset. Reduced from 48 in 1942-Q3 due to losses._
