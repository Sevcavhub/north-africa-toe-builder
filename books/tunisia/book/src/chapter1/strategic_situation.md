# Strategic Situation - 1943-Q1

## North Africa Theater

**Tunisia Campaign**
**Dates**: November 1942 - May 13, 1943
**Combatants**: American, British, Free French vs German, Italian

## Historical Context

Final campaign in North Africa. Axis trapped between two Allied armies. Kasserine Pass (Feb 1943) - American baptism of fire, tactical defeat but strategic lesson. Mareth Line breakthrough (Mar). Montgomery from east, Anderson/Patton from west. Axis supply cut by Malta-based air/naval forces. Von Arnim replaces Rommel (Mar). Final collapse May 1943 - 275,000 Axis prisoners.
