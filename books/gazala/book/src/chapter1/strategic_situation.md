# Strategic Situation - 1942-Q2

## North Africa Theater

**Battle of Gazala**
**Dates**: May 26 - June 21, 1942
**Combatants**: British, Commonwealth, Free French vs German, Italian

## Historical Context

Rommel's greatest victory. Sweeping left hook around Gazala Line defensive boxes. 'Cauldron' battle where Rommel consolidated behind British lines. Free French heroic defense at Bir Hacheim. British armor defeated piecemeal. Tobruk falls (June 21) with 33,000 prisoners. Rommel promoted to Field Marshal. Advance to El Alamein.
