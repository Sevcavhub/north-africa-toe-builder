# Appendix A: Quick Reference - Battle of Gazala

This appendix provides essential tables and references for quick gameplay during Battle of Gazala scenarios (May-June 1942).

## BattleGroup Core Rules Summary

### Turn Sequence

1. **Orders Phase**: Both players draw battle rating (BR) chits if needed, issue orders
2. **Aircraft Phase**: Resolve air strikes, air-to-air combat
3. **Initiative Phase**: Roll for initiative (higher roll chooses who activates first)
4. **Action Phase**: Alternate unit activations until both players pass
5. **Rally Phase**: Attempt to rally pinned units, repair breakdowns
6. **End Phase**: Remove transient markers, check victory conditions

### Key Mechanics

**Morale/Battle Rating**: Each force has BR (typically 30-60 points for battalion-level games). When you take a BR chit (from losses, objectives lost, etc.), draw randomly from your pool. Game ends when a side's BR is reduced to 0 or below.

**Orders/Command**: Most units need orders to act aggressively. Roll 2D6 vs command rating. Artillery always needs orders. Units within command range of HQ don't need to test for defensive actions.

**Movement Rates**: Infantry 5" per action, motorized 10", fast vehicles 12-16", slow tanks 8-10"

**Shooting**: Roll to hit (modified by range, cover, movement), then target rolls armor save (if armored) or cover save (if infantry/soft target). Penetrating hits on vehicles roll on damage table.

**Pinning**: Units taking fire that causes casualties become pinned. Pinned units have reduced effectiveness and must rally before moving or firing effectively.

## Special Rules Alphabetical Index

### A-C

**APCR Ammunition**: Limited tungsten-core rounds. +2 penetration up to 30". German 50mm and 75mm guns only. Max 3 shots per game.

**APDS Ammunition** (Experimental): Extremely rare British 6-pdr rounds. +3 penetration. Only 2 shots per gun per game, if available in scenario.

**Bir Hacheim Fortifications**: Free French defensive box. Hard cover (+2), extensive minefields, prepared positions for AT guns.

**Breakdown - Crusader**: Roll D6 when moving at full speed. On 1, immobilized. -1 modifier to all breakdown rolls.

**Breakdown - General**: D6 roll when moving at max speed over rough terrain. On 1, vehicle breaks down for D3 turns (1-4) or permanently (5-6).

**Breakdown - M13/40**: Roll at start of game. On 1-2, reduce movement by -4" all game (sand filters). -1 to breakdown rolls.

**Cauldron, The**: German defensive position. Units inside have hard cover (+2), limited supply (special scenario rules).

### D-H

**Desert Terrain**: Open terrain provides minimal cover. Most areas are open ground (no cover save) unless specifically marked.

**Dual Armament - Grant**: May fire both 75mm sponson and 37mm turret in same turn (different targets) or concentrate on one (+1 to hit).

**Dual Purpose (25-pdr)**: May fire HE (indirect artillery) or AP (direct AT). Switching modes requires 1 full action.

**Escarpment**: Impassable except at marked passes. Units on top get +6" spotting range. Reverse slope provides hard cover.

**Free French Forces**: Bir Hacheim garrison. High morale (+1), defend prepared positions, may sortie with limited armor.

**Grant High Profile**: Grant tanks cannot claim soft cover bonuses (only hard cover). Enemy spotters get +1 to detect.

**Grant Limited Traverse**: 75mm sponson has 30° traverse each side. Tank must face target. 37mm turret has full 360°.

**Hull-Down**: Tanks in hull-down positions get +1 armor save vs frontal shots.

### I-R

**Italian Morale**: Most Italian units have lower morale ratings. Use scenario-specific values (typically -1 vs German/British).

**Knightsbridge Box**: British defensive position. Prepared defenses, minefields, supply dumps.

**Mechanically Unreliable**: See Breakdown rules above.

**Minefield - Extensive**: Gazala featured massive British minefields. Movement through requires engineers. Roll D6: 1-2 = hit mine.

**New Zealand Forces**: 2nd NZ Division forces receive +1 to morale checks. Excellent combined arms coordination.

**Night Breakout - Bir Hacheim**: Visibility 12", close combat bonuses, infiltration possible. Used in final Free French breakout.

**No HE - 6-pounder**: Most 6-pdr guns lack HE ammunition in May-June 1942. Cannot engage infantry effectively.

**Panzer IV F2 Priority Target**: British forces must prioritize long 75mm Panzer IVs. When spotted, all AT assets check if can engage.

**Portee Mounting**: AT guns mounted on trucks. Move at truck speed but must dismount (1 action) to fire. Vulnerable when mounted.

### S-Z

**Semovente Low Profile**: Italian assault guns receive +1 to cover saves when hull-down (low silhouette).

**Smoke Rounds**: Create 6" diameter smoke screen, blocks LOS. Lasts 2 turns. Limited: British 4 rounds, German 2, Italian 1 per gun per game.

**South African Forces**: 1st and 2nd SA Divisions. Steady defensive troops, normal morale.

**Stuka Dive Bomber**: +1 to hit stationary/slow targets. Siren causes morale check (-1) to units within 12" of target.

**Supply Difficulties - Cauldron**: German units in Cauldron may face supply shortages. Special scenario rules for ammunition/fuel limits.

**Truck Vulnerability**: Trucks have no armor save. Any hit automatically damages the vehicle.

**Veteran Crews (German)**: German tank crews get +1 to shooting when firing first in an engagement. Represents superior training and experience.

**Wadi Crossing**: Only tracked vehicles may cross wadis. Roll D6: on 1-2, vehicle stuck and immobilized.

**Withdrawal Option**: In some scenarios, forces may voluntarily withdraw off table edge to preserve BR. Must exit designated edge.

### Vehicle Damage Table (D6)

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| **1** | Crew stunned - no permanent damage, pinned |
| **2-3** | Damaged - mobility or weapon hit, roll again for specific |
| **4-5** | Knocked out - vehicle immobilized, crew may bail out |
| **6** | Destroyed - catastrophic kill, vehicle explodes, crew casualties |

**Modifiers to damage roll**:
- Penetrated by 88mm or 75mm L/43: +1

- Hit by APCR/APDS: +1
- Ammunition explosion (Grant, vehicle with HE): On 6, +1 additional damage

## Force Organization Quick Reference

### British Force Structure (May-June 1942)

**Battalion-level game** (typical BR 35-45):
- 1x HQ (command value 8-9)
- 2-3x Infantry platoons (30-35 men each)
- 1x Support platoon (mortars, MGs, carriers)
- 1x Tank squadron (12-15 tanks: Grants, Crusaders, or Valentines)
- 1x Artillery battery (4-6 guns, typically 25-pdr)
- 1x AT gun section (2-4 guns, typically 6-pdr, few 2-pdr remaining)

**Grant Squadrons**: Mix of Grant (75mm+37mm) and Stuart (37mm) tanks. Usually 9-12 Grants, 3-4 Stuarts per squadron.

### German Force Structure (May-June 1942)

**Battalion-level game** (typical BR 35-45):
- 1x HQ (command value 9-10, veteran)
- 2-3x Panzergrenadier platoons (30-35 men each)
- 1x Support platoon (mortars, MG34s, PAK)
- 1x Panzer company (12-18 tanks: Panzer III/IV mix)
- 1x Artillery battery (4-6 guns, 105mm or 88mm)
- 1x AT gun section (2-4 guns, PAK 38/PAK 40, or 88mm)

**Panzer Company Typical Mix**: 10-12x Panzer III (50mm L/60), 2-4x Panzer IV F2 (75mm L/43), 0-2x Panzer III N (short 75mm support).

### Italian Force Structure (May-June 1942)

**Battalion-level game** (typical BR 28-38):
- 1x HQ (command value 7-8)
- 3-4x Infantry platoons (30-35 men each)
- 1x Support platoon (mortars, Breda HMGs)
- 1x Tank/Assault Gun company (8-12 M13/40, M14/41, or Semovente)
- 1x Artillery battery (4-6 guns, 75mm)
- 1x AT gun section (4-6 guns, 47mm, rare 90mm if available)

**Ariete Division**: Elite Italian armored division. Better morale (+1) than standard Italian units. M13/40 and M14/41 equipped.

### Free French Force Structure (Bir Hacheim, June 1942)

**Battalion-level game** (typical BR 32-42):
- 1x HQ (command value 8)
- 2-3x Infantry platoons (30-35 men each, mixed French Colonial troops)
- 1x Support platoon (mortars, MGs, 75mm guns)
- 1x Artillery battery (4-6x 75mm guns in prepared positions)
- 1x AT gun section (4-6x 25-pdr in AT role, or captured weapons)
- Limited armor: Few Bren carriers, 1-2 armored cars

**High Morale**: Free French forces at Bir Hacheim receive +1 to morale checks. Determined defense.

## Combat Example: Grant vs Panzer IV F2 at "The Cauldron"

**Situation**: American Grant tank (51mm frontal armor, 75mm+37mm guns) engages German Panzer IV F2 (50mm+ frontal armor, 75mm L/43 gun) at 28" range.

**Turn 1**: Both tanks spot each other in open desert. German fires first (veteran crew).

**German Panzer IV F2 fires at Grant**:
- To Hit Roll: Base 4+, range 28" (no penalty under 30"), target in open: +0, veteran crew first shot: +1
- **Result: Needs 3+ to hit**
- German rolls 4 - **HIT**

**Penetration**:
- 75mm L/43 penetration @ 28": 90mm (vs Grant 51mm armor)
- Grant armor save: Base 4+
- 75mm L/43 powerful gun: -1
- **Final save needed: 5+**

British rolls 3 - **PENETRATED**

**Damage Roll**:
- Base D6 roll: 5
- Powerful 75mm L/43: +1
- **Final: 6 = Destroyed (catastrophic)**

**Result**: Grant destroyed, crew casualties, British player draws BR chit.

**British Response** (if Grant had fired both guns):

**Grant fires 75mm at Panzer IV F2**:
- To Hit: Base 4+, range 28": +0
- British rolls 5 - **HIT**

**Penetration**:
- Grant 75mm M3 penetration @ 28": 65mm (vs Panzer IV F2 50mm+ frontal)
- Panzer IV armor save: Base 4+, improved F2 armor: +1
- **Final save needed: 5+**

German rolls 5 - **ARMOR HOLDS**

**Grant fires 37mm at Panzer IV F2** (turret gun):
- To Hit: Base 4+, dual target concentration: +1
- British rolls 6 - **HIT**

**Penetration**:
- 37mm M6 penetration @ 28": 40mm (vs Panzer IV 50mm+ armor)
- Panzer IV armor save: Base 4+, improved armor: +1
- **Final save needed: 5+ (difficult)**

German rolls 3 - **PENETRATED**

**Damage Roll**:
- Base D6 roll: 3
- No modifiers (small caliber)
- **Result: 3 = Damaged**

**Result**: Panzer IV damaged (mobility or weapon hit), German player draws BR chit.

**Historical Note**: The Panzer IV F2 with long 75mm gun was devastating against British armor at Gazala. Grant tanks provided the first effective HE capability for British armor but were vulnerable to the new German gun. The dual armament sometimes allowed Grants to score hits even when the 75mm failed to penetrate.

## Battle of Gazala Key Equipment Summary

### British Strengths (May-June 1942)
- **Grant tanks**: First effective HE + dual armament flexibility
- **6-pdr AT guns**: Finally can penetrate German armor
- **American reliability**: Grant and Stuart mechanically sound
- **25-pdr versatility**: Dual-purpose artillery/AT
- **Defensive positions**: Bir Hacheim, Knightsbridge boxes well-prepared

### British Weaknesses
- **Grant high profile**: Difficult to conceal, limited traverse
- **Crusader still unreliable**: ~30% mechanical breakdown rate
- **6-pdr lacks HE**: Cannot engage infantry (yet)
- **Scattered positions**: Boxes isolated, hard to support

### German Strengths
- **Panzer IV F2**: Game-changing long 75mm gun, dual capable
- **Veteran crews**: Superior training and combat experience
- **75mm PaK 40**: Powerful new AT gun appearing
- **88mm dominance**: Still devastating vs all Allied tanks
- **Tactical flexibility**: Mobile warfare, combined arms mastery

### German Weaknesses
- **Limited Panzer IV F2**: Only ~40 available at start of Gazala
- **Supply difficulties**: Extended lines, vulnerable convoys
- **Outnumbered**: British numerical superiority in tanks

### Italian Weaknesses
- **M13/40 obsolete**: Cannot penetrate Grant or Valentine
- **47mm inadequate**: AT gun useless vs British armor
- **Low morale**: Most units less effective than German/British
- **Mechanical issues**: M13/40 sand filter problems persist

### Free French (Bir Hacheim)
- **High morale**: Determined defense, +1 morale
- **Prepared defenses**: Extensive fortifications and minefields
- **Limited armor**: Few AFVs, mostly infantry/artillery
- **Isolated**: Difficult to resupply or reinforce
