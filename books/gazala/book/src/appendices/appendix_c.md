# Appendix C: Historical Sources

This appendix documents the primary and secondary sources used to construct the Gazala scenarios, orders of battle, and equipment datacards. The Battle of Gazala (May-June 1942) was Rommel's masterpiece - the "Gazala Gallop" - featuring the epic Free French defense at Bir Hacheim, the "Cauldron" battles, and the fall of Tobruk.

---

## Primary Sources

### War Diaries and Official Records

1. **War Diary, 1st Armoured Division, May-June 1942** (TNA WO 169/4365)
   - British National Archives, Kew
   - Grant tank combat reports from Knightsbridge battles
   - Tank strength returns during Cauldron engagements
   - First use of Grant M3 with 75mm gun in major battle

2. **War Diary, 50th Infantry Division, May-June 1942** (TNA WO 169/4482)
   - 150th Brigade Box siege and destruction
   - Gazala line defensive positions and minefields
   - Final withdrawal from Gazala line ("Gazala Gallop")

3. **War Diary, 7th Armoured Division, May-June 1942** (TNA WO 169/4350)
   - Desert Rats in Gazala battles
   - Knightsbridge tank engagements
   - Withdrawal to Egyptian frontier

4. **Journal de Marche, 1re Brigade Française Libre, Mai-Juin 1942**
   - Service Historique de la Défense, Vincennes, France
   - Bir Hacheim siege documentation (May 27 - June 10, 1942)
   - Free French defensive operations and breakout
   - Most detailed French account of desert war's finest hour

5. **Kriegstagebuch, Panzergruppe Afrika, Mai-Juni 1942**
   - Bundesarchiv RH 21-3/v.30-31
   - Rommel's "left hook" planning and execution
   - Cauldron battles and breakout from encirclement
   - Tobruk assault planning and execution

6. **Kriegstagebuch, 15. Panzer-Division, Mai-Juni 1942**
   - Bundesarchiv RH 27-15/5
   - Panzer III Ausf J (long 50mm) and Panzer IV F2 (long 75mm) combat reports
   - Knightsbridge tank battles
   - 150th Brigade Box assault operations

7. **Kriegstagebuch, 21. Panzer-Division, Mai-Juni 1942**
   - Bundesarchiv RH 27-21/3
   - Left-hook maneuver south of Bir Hacheim
   - Cauldron defensive operations
   - Pursuit to Egyptian frontier

8. **Kriegstagebuch, 90. leichte Afrika-Division, Mai-Juni 1942**
   - Bundesarchiv RH 27-90/4
   - Bir Hacheim siege operations
   - Motorized infantry operations in Cauldron
   - Tobruk assault (northern sector)

9. **Diario Storico, Divisione Corazzata Ariete, Maggio-Giugno 1942**
   - Ufficio Storico dello Stato Maggiore dell'Esercito, Rome
   - Bir Hacheim first assaults (May 27-28)
   - Cauldron battles alongside DAK
   - M13/40 tank performance against Grant tanks

10. **Diario Storico, Divisione Corazzata Littorio, Maggio-Giugno 1942**
    - Ufficio Storico SME, Rome
    - Italian armored division debut in North Africa
    - Coordination with German Panzer divisions
    - Equipment: M14/41 tanks, Semovente 75/18

## Secondary Sources

### Major Historical Works

25. **Macksey, Kenneth. "The Battle of Gazala"**

    - Most comprehensive single-volume study of Gazala

    - 150th Brigade Box siege documented

26. **Lyman, Robert. "Tobruk 1942: Rommel and the Defeat of the Allies"**
    - Osprey Publishing, Oxford, 2011 (Campaign 248)
    - Modern scholarly study

    - 33,000 prisoners and massive supply capture

27. **Carrell, Paul. "The Foxes of the Desert"**
    - E.P. Dutton, New York, 1961 (originally "Die Wüstenfüchse", 1958)
    - German perspective on North Africa campaign
    - Bir Hacheim siege from German viewpoint
    - Rommel's "left hook" maneuver described

28. **Pitt, Barrie. "The Crucible of War: Year of Alamein 1942"**
    - Jonathan Cape, London, 1980

    - Grant tank introduction and performance

29. **Barr, Niall. "Pendulum of War: The Three Battles of El Alamein"**

30. **Jentz, Thomas L. "Panzertruppen"** (1933-1942 volume)

31. **Walker, Ian W. "Iron Hulls, Iron Hearts"**

### Equipment and Technical References

37. **Chamberlain, Peter and Ellis, Chris. "British and American Tanks of World War II"**
    - Arms and Armour Press, London, 1969

38. **Jentz, Thomas L. "Panzerkampfwagen IV Ausf.A to J"** (Panzer Tracts No.4)

    - Combat debut at Gazala (May 1942)

39. **Jentz, Thomas L. "Panzerkampfwagen III Ausf.A to N"** (Panzer Tracts No.3-2)

    - Panzer III Ausf J (long 50mm) standard at Gazala

## Source Quality Assessment

### Tier 1 Primary Sources (95-100% Confidence)
- War diaries (TNA WO series, Bundesarchiv RH series, French SHD)
- Official histories (Playfair, Agar-Hamilton/Turner, Broche)
- British Army Lists and German KStN documents
- Tessin divisional histories
- Nafziger Collection Gazala OOBs
- Free French Archives de la France Libre

### Tier 2 Specialist Sources (85-95% Confidence)
- Jentz "Panzertruppen" and Panzer Tracts series
- Walker "Iron Hulls, Iron Hearts" (Italian forces)
- Zaloga M3 Grant tank studies
- Macksey "The Battle of Gazala"
- Ford "Gazala 1942" (Osprey)
- Broche & Caïtucoli "Bir Hakeim" (2002)

### Tier 3 General Histories (70-85% Confidence)
- Pitt, Barr, Moorehead narrative histories
- Young "Rommel: The Desert Fox"
- Carrell "Foxes of the Desert"
- Memoirs (Crisp, Schmidt) - colorful but verify tactical details

### Not Used (Unreliable)
- Wikipedia (too variable in quality, not citable)
- Amateur websites without source citations
- Forum posts and discussions (useful for research leads only)

## Gaps and Limitations

### Known Data Gaps
1. **Free French equipment details**: Limited archival access, some equipment estimates
2. **Italian armored divisions**: Littorio Division debut, limited primary sources
3. **150th Brigade Box final days**: Some defender accounts lost (brigade destroyed)
4. **Tank operational readiness**: Daily fluctuations during multi-week campaign

### Confidence Levels by Category
- **British ground forces**: 85-90% (good Army Lists, war diaries, but Gazala defeat caused records loss)
- **German ground forces**: 88-92% (excellent Tessin/Nafziger, KTB access)
- **Free French forces**: 82-88% (French archives excellent for Bir Hacheim, limited elsewhere)
- **Italian forces**: 78-85% (limited primary sources, reliance on secondary works)
- **Equipment specifications**: 90-95% (OnWar, WWIITANKS databases excellent)

### Research Recommendations
For researchers seeking to expand on this work:
1. Visit Service Historique de la Défense (Vincennes) for Free French records
2. Bundesarchiv-Militärarchiv (Freiburg) for German Panzerarmee Afrika war diaries
3. TNA Kew for British 1st Armoured Division and 50th Division records
4. Ufficio Storico (Rome) for Italian Ariete and Littorio Division archives
5. Cross-reference Grant M3 combat reports with German tank kill claims
