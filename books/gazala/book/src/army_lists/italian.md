### Tanks

**Total**: 160

#### Medium Tanks

- **M13/40 Medium Tank**: 48 total (36 operational) - _Main battle tank declining in numbers as M14/41 replacements arrive. 14 tons, crew 4. 125 hp diesel engine chronically underpowered. Transmission failures remain frequent. Adequate vs British light tanks but comprehensively outclassed by new M3 Grant medium tanks (75mm gun, 50mm armor) arriving with British Eighth Army May-June 1942. Effective range vs armor: 500m._
- **M14/41 Medium Tank**: 37 total (32 operational) - _Improved M13/40 with 145 hp diesel engine and better air filters for desert. 14.5 tons, crew 4. Now standard issue, replacing M13/40 during Q2 1942 refitting. Mechanically more reliable than M13/40 but still inadequate vs British medium/heavy armor. Better performance in desert heat with improved cooling and filtration. Constituted ~44% of medium tank strength by June 1942._

#### Light Tanks

- **L6/40 Light Tank**: 75 total (54 operational) - _Light reconnaissance tank. 6.8 tons, crew 2. 70 hp engine. 42 km/h speed. Used for scouting, flank security. Ineffective vs British armor but good against soft targets and reconnaissance. Two reconnaissance battalions in 32nd Light Armored Regiment. Operational strength increased from Q1 due to new deliveries and improved maintenance during static Gazala period._
