🎨 Army List Datacard Generator

Generate BattleGroup datacards from OSJones Builder army lists

📋 How to use:

  1. Visit OSJones BattleGroup Builder
  2. Create your army list
  3. Click "Print" button
  4. Copy all text (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C)
  5. Paste into the box below and click Generate!
