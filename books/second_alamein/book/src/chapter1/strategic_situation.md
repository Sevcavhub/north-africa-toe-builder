# Strategic Situation - 1942-Q4

## North Africa Theater

**Second Battle of El Alamein**
**Dates**: October 23 - November 11, 1942
**Combatants**: British, Commonwealth, South African, Indian, New Zealand, Australian, Free French, Greek vs German, Italian

## Historical Context

Montgomery's set-piece breakthrough battle. Operation Lightfoot (infantry phase) followed by Supercharge (armor breakthrough). Overwhelming British material superiority (195,000 vs 104,000 men, 1,029 vs 547 tanks). Hitler's 'stand or die' order ignored. Rommel's long retreat begins. Churchill: 'This is not the end, not even the beginning of the end, but perhaps the end of the beginning.'
