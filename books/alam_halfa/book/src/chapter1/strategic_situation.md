# Strategic Situation - 1942-Q3

## North Africa Theater

**Battle of Alam Halfa**
**Dates**: August 30 - September 5, 1942
**Combatants**: British, Commonwealth, South African, New Zealand vs German, Italian

## Historical Context

Montgomery's first battle as 8th Army commander. Rommel's final offensive attempt before British reinforcements arrive. British fight defensive battle from prepared positions on Alam Halfa ridge. RAF air superiority dominant. German fuel shortages critical. Rommel forced to retreat. Turning point - Axis never offensive again in North Africa.
