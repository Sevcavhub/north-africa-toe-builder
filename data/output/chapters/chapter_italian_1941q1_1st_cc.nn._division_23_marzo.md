## 11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

### Doctrinal Role

Final defense of Bardia fortress perimeter - DIVISION DESTROYED. The 1st CCNN Division '23 Marzo' held the central 'Ponticelli' sector of Bardia defenses alongside elements of 62nd Infantry Division 'Marmarica'. Under siege from 1 December 1940 until destruction on 5 January 1941. Part of XXIII Corps (General Annibale Bergonzoli) defending Bardia against Australian 6th Division.

- Political reliability (Fascist Party militia)
