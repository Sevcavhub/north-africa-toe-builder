## 11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

### Doctrinal Role

Commonwealth infantry division in TRANSITION to Western Desert operations. Q2 1941 mission: April-May (training in South Africa for North African deployment), June (sea deployment to Egypt), late June-August (desert warfare training at El Alamein, defensive works construction). Division formed October 23, 1940 under Maj-Gen de Villiers. NOT operationally deployed in combat during Q2 1941.

### Special Capabilities

- Motorized infantry division with good mobility
- South African armoured car reconnaissance
- Commonwealth combined arms doctrine

### Tactical Innovations

- Integration of SA Police battalions (6th Brigade)
- Marmon-Herrington armoured cars for reconnaissance
