## 11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

### Doctrinal Role

Operational-level formation responsible for coordinating combined-arms offensive to relieve Tobruk and recapture Cyrenaica from Axis forces. Executed Operation Battleaxe (15-17 June 1941).

### Special Capabilities

- Combined arms coordination (armour, infantry, artillery)
- Desert warfare operations
- Siege relief operations (Tobruk garrison)
- Mobile operations in open desert terrain

### Tactical Innovations

- First large-scale use of Crusader cruiser tanks in North Africa
- Attempted coordination between slow Matilda infantry tanks (8 mph) and faster Crusader cruisers (27 mph)

### Known Issues and Limitations

- Tank speed disparity: Matilda II infantry tanks too slow (8 mph max) for desert mobile warfare
- 2-pounder anti-tank guns inadequate penetration vs German 50mm frontal armor
- British tanks vulnerable to German 88mm AA guns in anti-tank role
- Slow command reaction time vs German tactical flexibility
- Inadequate radio communication between armoured brigades
- Fuel and ammunition logistics strained by 600km supply line

### Desert Adaptations

Water rationing (4L/day/person), vehicle maintenance against sand infiltration, navigation by sun compass and dead reckoning
