## 11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

### Doctrinal Role

Static defensive operations anchoring southern flank of Eighth Army Gazala Line. Pivot point for Allied maneuver in Libyan Desert. Defended fortified position (Bir Hakeim) with extensive minefield network.

### Special Capabilities

- Desert warfare experience from previous operations in Libya, Eritrea, Syria, Gabon
- Expertise in defensive fortification and minefield construction
- Multi-national colonial composition (French, Foreign Legion, North African, Equatorial African, Pacific Islander)
- Integration with British Eighth Army command structure
- Night breakout operations and infiltration tactics

### Tactical Innovations

- Extensive minefield defensive system with five entrances/chicanes
- All vehicles and positions dug-in below ground level for protection
- Integration of artillery in anti-tank role at close range (75mm guns)
- Coordinated all-arms defense (infantry, artillery, anti-tank, anti-aircraft)
- Organized breakout under fire through encirclement on night of June 10-11
