## 11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

### Doctrinal Role

Mobile light infantry division for deep desert operations, flank security, and pursuit operations. Lacked heavy armor but compensated with firepower from artillery and anti-tank guns. Designed for rapid movement and exploitation.

### Special Capabilities

- Desert mobility and navigation
- Independent operations far from supply bases
- Rapid deployment and concentration
- Combined arms coordination with Italian mobile forces

### Tactical Innovations

- Use of captured enemy equipment to supplement shortages
- Integration of improvised self-propelled guns (Diana)
- Adaptive logistics using mixed vehicle fleet

### Known Issues and Limitations

- Chronic shortage of German vehicles - heavy reliance on captured British trucks
- No organic tank strength (only 2-3 StuG IIIs)
- Vulnerable to enemy armor without close support from Panzer divisions
- Long supply lines from Tripoli/Benghazi causing fuel and ammunition shortages
- Insufficient anti-aircraft protection against British air superiority

### Desert Adaptations

Extensive use of water discipline, desert camouflage, and adapted maintenance procedures for vehicles operating in sand and extreme heat. Navigation by sun compass and dead reckoning.
