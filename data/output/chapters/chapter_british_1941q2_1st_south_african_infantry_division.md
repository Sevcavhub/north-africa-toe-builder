## 11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

### Doctrinal Role

Standard Commonwealth infantry division conducting mountain warfare in East African campaign. Mission in Q2 1941: pursuit of Italian forces north from Addis Ababa toward Amba Alagi. Division executed rapid advance covering 2,735km in 53 days (January-April 1941).

### Special Capabilities

- Mountain warfare experience from Ethiopian highlands campaign
- Rapid motorized movement across difficult terrain
- Combined arms cooperation with East African forces

### Tactical Innovations

- Use of South African armoured cars for long-range reconnaissance
- Integration with East African brigades (21st/22nd/25th EA Brigades at various times)
