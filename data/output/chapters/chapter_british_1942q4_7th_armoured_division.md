## 11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

### Doctrinal Role

Mobile armoured exploitation force. Specialized in wide flanking movements, pursuit operations, and reconnaissance in force. 'Desert Rats' nickname earned through aggressive armoured warfare in North African campaign.

### Special Capabilities

- Rapid pursuit and exploitation
- Wide desert flanking movements
- Combined arms coordination (armour, infantry, artillery)
- Extended desert operations with minimal supply
- Night movement and navigation
- Tank gunnery excellence

### Tactical Innovations

- Leaguer defensive formations in desert
- Mixed tank squadrons (Sherman/Grant heavy + Stuart light)
- Motor infantry battalion integration with armoured brigades
- Mobile artillery support using portee-mounted guns
- Radio net coordination for dispersed operations

### Known Issues and Limitations

- Extended supply lines during pursuit (1800km from Alexandria)
- Tank mechanical reliability during high-tempo operations
- Fuel and water constraints limiting operational radius
- Mixed tank types complicating maintenance and logistics
- Vulnerability to German 88mm anti-tank guns

### Desert Adaptations

Extensive desert warfare modifications: sun compasses, sand channels, condensers for water recovery, extra water and fuel stowage, desert camouflage patterns, goggles and respirators for dust storms, specialized navigation training. Unit pioneered desert warfare tactics 1940-1942.
