## 8. Supply and Logistics

Complete collapse. Division operating at catastrophic supply levels during Second Battle of El Alamein (October-November 1942). Water supply critically below survival minimum: 2.0L per day per man (vs. 3.5L minimum desert requirement) = 20,700L daily for 10,350 men.
