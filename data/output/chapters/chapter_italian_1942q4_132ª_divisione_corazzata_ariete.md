## 11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

### Doctrinal Role

Armored spearhead and mobile reserve for XX Corpo d'Armata. Counterattack force against British breakthrough attempts. Desert warfare specialists with 21 months continuous combat experience.

### Special Capabilities

- Desert navigation and survival skills
- Coordination with German Afrika Korps
- Night movement and concealment
- Rapid mine-laying and breaching

### Tactical Innovations

- Use of 88mm AA guns in anti-tank role
- Integration of Semovente SP guns with tank battalions
- Defensive box formations pioneered in desert warfare

### Combined Arms Integration

**[MANUAL: Add section about infantry-tank-artillery cooperation, air support, engineer support]**

### Known Issues and Limitations

- M13/40 and M14/41 tanks inferior to British Grants and Shermans in armor and firepower
- 47mm AT guns inadequate against British cruiser tanks
- Chronic fuel shortage limiting mobility
- Insufficient recovery and repair vehicles
- Radio equipment unreliable in desert conditions
- Truck shortages limiting supply capacity

### Desert Adaptations

Extensive desert modifications: dust filters on engines, sand tires, extra water and fuel storage, sun camouflage schemes, enlarged radiators. Crews highly experienced but equipment worn from 21 months continuous desert operations.
