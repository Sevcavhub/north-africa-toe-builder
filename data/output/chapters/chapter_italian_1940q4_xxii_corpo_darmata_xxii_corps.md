## 8. Supply and Logistics

### Supply Status

General reserve corps at Tobruk fortress. Best supply situation of all 10th Army corps due to direct port access at Tobruk. Fuel reserves excellent (15-18 days), ammunition stocks very good (20-25 days), water supply from Tobruk port/wells adequate. XXII Corps included fortress troops and heavy artillery for Tobruk defense. However, corps held in general reserve and not committed during Italian invasion of Egypt (Sept 1940) or initial Sidi Barrani deployment. When Operation Compass launched (9 Dec 1940), XXII Corps remained at Tobruk while forward corps destroyed.
