## 11. Tactical Doctrine and Capabilities

### Doctrinal Role

First US Army division to conduct opposed amphibious landing in European Theater. Assaulted Vichy French-defended Oran on November 8, 1942 (Operation Torch). Triangular division (3 infantry regiments vs. old square 4-regiment structure). Emphasis on combined arms, fire and maneuver, and aggressive patrolling.

### Special Capabilities

- Amphibious assault trained (though limited pre-invasion preparation)
- First US division with M1 Garand semi-automatic rifle as primary weapon
- Combined arms coordination at regimental level
- Mechanized reconnaissance capability

### Tactical Innovations

- Triangular division structure (more flexible than square divisions)
- Integrated anti-tank defense at battalion level
- Mobile artillery support with motorized transport

**[MANUAL: Add section about infantry-tank-artillery cooperation, air support, engineer support]**

### Known Issues and Limitations

- Limited combat experience (first action for most personnel)
- 37mm anti-tank gun inadequate against German armor (discovered later in Tunisia)
- Training focused on open terrain; limited urban combat preparation
- Coordination issues between landing craft crews and infantry during Oran assault

### Desert Adaptations

Minimal desert-specific training prior to Torch. Coastal assault focus with Mediterranean climate rather than Saharan desert operations. Post-landing adapted with water discipline, vehicle maintenance for dust, and supply line extension.
