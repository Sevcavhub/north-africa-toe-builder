Comprehensive research citations for the North Africa Campaign Books

About the Research

Each book in the North Africa Campaign series is meticulously researched using primary sources, official military records, and authoritative historical references. This project draws from 181 Phase 6 unit extraction citations and 71 archive references, ensuring historical accuracy in equipment specifications, organizational structures, and tactical scenarios.

The bibliography for each book is contained in Appendix C: Historical Sources, documenting all references used for that specific battle or campaign. Click the links below to access the complete citations for each book.

Source Categories

Primary Sources: War diaries, after-action reports, unit records, and official military histories from British, German, Italian, and American archives.

Secondary Sources: Academic monographs, regimental histories, and authoritative reference works by military historians.

Technical References: Equipment specifications from Jane's Fighting Vehicles, WWIITANKS database (612 AFVs), OnWar military encyclopedia, and official BattleGroup game supplements.

Database Integration: This project synthesizes data from 469 equipment items, 402 unit-quarter extractions, and 1,296 penetration data points to provide historically accurate game statistics.
