## 🇬🇧 British Infantry Organization (1941-1942)

### Battalion Structure
- **4 Rifle Companies** (3 platoons each)
- **1 Headquarters Company**
  - Anti-Aircraft Platoon
  - Anti-Tank Platoon
  - Carrier Platoon
  - **NO Vickers machine guns** (Middle East variation)

### Rifle Company
- **3 Platoons** (36-40 men each)
- Company HQ
- Total: ~120-130 men

### Rifle Platoon (36-40 men)
**Platoon HQ:**
- Lieutenant (Platoon Commander)
- Platoon Sergeant
- Batman (Commander's aide)
- Runner/Signaler
- 2-inch Mortar Crew (3 men)

**3 Rifle Sections** (10 men each):
- Corporal (Section Leader)
- Lance Corporal (Assistant)
- 8 Riflemen (6 active + 2 reserve)
- **1 Bren Light Machine Gun** (primary firepower)
- Lee-Enfield No. 4 Rifles
- Grenades (Mills bombs)

### Weapons Summary
- **Per Section**: 1x Bren LMG, 9x Lee-Enfield rifles
- **Per Platoon**: 3x Bren LMG, 1x 2-inch mortar, ~30x rifles
- **Per Company**: 9x Bren LMG, 3x 2-inch mortars

### BattleGroup Points Estimate (British Platoon)
- Platoon HQ: ~15 points
- 3x Rifle Sections (10 men each): ~120 points (40 each)
- 2-inch Mortar: ~15 points
- **Total: ~150-170 points**

For 400-600 point games: 2-3 platoons + support weapons

## 🇩🇪 German Infantry Organization (Afrika Korps 1941-1942)

### Special Afrika Organization (KStN 1114 Afrika)
**Key Adaptation**: 'Stutzpunkt' (strongpoint) organization
- Higher proportion of MGs and AT guns vs. European theater
- All-round defense capability (mobile Kampfgruppen)
- Light mortars **discarded** (useless in open desert)

### Platoon Structure (Modified for Africa)
**Platoon HQ:**
- Officer
- Platoon Sergeant
- Runners/Signalers

**3-4 Squads** (10 men each):
- Squad Leader (Unteroffizier)
- **2 Halves** (5 men each)
  - Half 1: MG42/MG34 team
  - Half 2: Rifle team (Kar 98k)
- Fire-and-maneuver tactics

**Anti-Tank Section** (per platoon):
- 1x Anti-Tank Rifle (Panzerbüchse 38/39, 7.92mm)
- **2x Anti-Tank Guns**:
  - PaK 38 (5cm) - early
  - Captured Soviet 76.2mm PaK 36(r) - 1942+

### Weapons Summary
- **Per Squad**: 1-2x MG34/MG42 LMG, 8x Kar 98k rifles
- **Per Platoon**: 6-8x MG34/MG42, 1x AT rifle, 2x PaK guns
- **Total**: ~40-50 men per platoon (including AT section)

### BattleGroup Points Estimate (German Platoon)
- Platoon HQ: ~20 points
- 3x Infantry Squads (10 men, 2x MG): ~180 points (60 each)
- AT Rifle Team: ~20 points
- 2x PaK 38 (5cm): ~80 points (40 each)
- **Total: ~300-320 points**

For 400-600 point games: 1 platoon + AT support + additional infantry OR 2 platoons without heavy AT

## 🇮🇹 Italian Infantry Organization (1941-1942)

### Battalion Structure
- **3 Rifle Companies**
- **1 Weapons Company** (heavy weapons)

### Rifle Company (Binary Organization)
- **3 Platoons** (20 men each)
- Company HQ
- Total: ~70-80 men

### Rifle Platoon (20 men) - BINARY SYSTEM
**Platoon HQ:**
- Lieutenant
- Platoon Sergeant

**2 Sections** (10 men each):
Each section divided into **2 specialized squads**:

1. **Fire Squad** (5 men):
   - Squad Leader
   - **2x Breda Mod. 1930 LMGs**
   - 2 Ammo Carriers
   - Flank Security

2. **Maneuver Squad** (5 men):
   - Squad Leader
   - 4 Riflemen (Carcano M1891 rifles)
   - Grenades (Breda Mod. 35)

### Weapons Summary
- **Per Section**: 2x Breda Mod. 1930 LMG, 5x Carcano rifles
- **Per Platoon**: 4x Breda LMG, 10x rifles
- **Per Company**: 12x Breda LMG, 30x rifles

### Support Weapons (Regiment Level)
- **Guns Company**: 4x 65/17mm Infantry Guns (per regiment)

### BattleGroup Points Estimate (Italian Platoon)
- Platoon HQ: ~10 points
- 2x Sections (10 men, 2x Breda LMG each): ~100 points (50 each)
- **Total: ~110-130 points**

For 400-600 point games: 3-4 platoons + support weapons (65/17mm guns, mortars)

**Note**: Italian forces often understrength and poorly equipped. Historical accuracy suggests lower points values and morale penalties.
